# CG video story editor

The editor remains accountable for the story and final cut. Use AI and automation to find footage, test structures, mark beats and check technical details. Start with the exact client's brief, existing Content Guideline, authorised OneDrive media and relevant CG final exports. Respect the existing client content and approval authorities; this skill creates no new production task or approval state.

1. State the buyer, relevant moment, proof and call to action in one sentence. Screen original footage as moving portrait crops; label genuine, stock and archival material. Do not imply that separate projects or illustrative scenes are one installation.
2. Watch relevant CG final exports with sound and record precise timecoded examples of opening, shot scale, movement, rhythm, effects, sound and outro. Copy the *decision principle* when it fits this client's story, not the edit itself.
3. Build the music and sound map before finalizing shot lengths. Choose whether the opening is song-led or SFX-led; an SFX rush can resolve into a hold before the song begins. Use audible beat and phrase markers as candidates, then make cuts where action and meaning land. For opening architecture, match-motion, J/L cuts and reusable cue selection, read the [rhythm and sound playbook](references/rhythm-sound-playbook.md) and [CG sound design catalog](references/sfx-library.md).
4. Keep picture, title, music, sound effects, genuine camera sound and brand sound identifiable in the Premiere timeline. Sound may lead or outlast a picture cut. Retain originals and effect settings when stabilization, ramps or transitions are baked into processed picture clips.
5. Selectively stabilize only distracting jitter; compare the moving result with the original for warped edges, floating objects and crop. Give the outcome and product proof enough time to register after faster sections.
6. Export a review version and an online editable Premiere project. Check the entire render at phone size with and without sound, decode and dimensions, text-safe area, contact details, audio balance, rights, prior use and final brand approval. A technical pass is not an editorial or release approval.
7. After each revision, record the specific viewer/editor feedback, change and evidence. Add only reusable client-agnostic lessons to the playbook; keep client media, private links and credentials out of GitHub.

`python scripts/screen_sources.py <input-folder> <output-folder>` can generate triage frames and a source ledger from local originals with FFmpeg. Watch shortlisted clips in motion before selecting them.

Optional vendor tools and workstation licensing follow [issue #856](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/issues/856). Do not install or claim them from this skill alone.
