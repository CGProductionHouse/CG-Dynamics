# Load once, choose craft, execute the actual story

CA6November asks for a faster foundation: monthly SouthAfrican context plus the
existing client game plan and approved design floor, followed by a small
industry-specific refresh. This is the entry into the existing story/production
contract, not another board, research engine, chat brain or effects quota.

1. **Load the exact established client.** Resolve Dynamics identity and use
   `load_client_library.py`. Reuse known files, fonts, actual Canva assets,
   official outro and prior reference films. Record approval scope: typography
   approval does not approve every opening, and CA approval is not publication.
   `CLIENT_CONTEXT.json` can bind `baseline_file` to a relative-path/hash-checked
   `brand/CURRENT-APPROVED-BASELINE.json`. Superseded/rejected assets stay in the
   archive but are excluded from active selection. A missing/changed known
   asset needs bounded recovery, not a general brand hunt or plain-font fallback.
2. **Load the monthly context and exact Dynamics game plan.** Reuse
   [November research](sa-context-2026-11.json). Call the read-only
   `get_monthly_strategy_brief` for the exact client/month as well as its current
   task-scoped client context. Save source ID/version/state/fingerprint; a draft
   or `EXACT_STRATEGY_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE` remains explicit. Do not overwrite a
   staff-amended strategy, call a paid content generator or substitute a sibling.
   Same-client strategy must still match this month; refresh its fingerprint/state
   once per task/day (24h snapshot), not the underlying brand research.
   New owner instructions can supply direction while that evidence gap is recorded.
3. **Make the small fresh decision.** Select a current, relevant dated angle or
   deliberately choose evergreen with a reason. Then check only the industry's
   changed facts, recent posts, actual service/offer/source coverage and this
   assignment. Never force politics, holidays or a weather claim into everyfilm.
4. **Choose from the actual library.** `edit_foundation.py` emits all current
   card names/descriptions/hashes and implementation locations with the loaded
   inputs. Read the few relevant cards' real prerequisites. Decide explicitly
   for typography, composition/motion, transitions, UI and sound: use/omit,
   specific mechanism, scene/purpose, adjustable parameters, control, risks and
   fallback. Carry selected visual IDs into `skill_applications` in the SAME
   story plan. Sound needs actual licensed/source-aligned cues and the music-use
   exclusion ledger; a whoosh at everycut is not sound design.
5. **Design0–3s before a full film.** Storyboard an observable event/change,
   viewer question and payoff. A static portrait with a premium question was
   explicitly rejected as a future hook even after PSGR08 film approval.
   Develop actual source action, an information transformation, anticipation or
   contrast; a rendered title entrance is not automatically an effective hook.
   Compare moving plain/designed openings, inspect reduced/full-size type and
   actual sound. Keep genuine results and licensed sources separate from claims.
6. **Execute and retain the improvement.** Existing plan→implementation→plain
   control→exact-export checks still apply. Inspect the whole output and every
   revised word/face/crop, motion, beat relationship and exact sound. At approval,
   update BOTH structured current state and readable notes, add exact reference
   hashes and field-level keep/change/reject decisions. Do not leave an old
   rejected `current_candidate_state` or rejected graphic labelled approved.

The command loads evidence; it cannot finish the creative decisions:

`python scripts/edit_foundation.py --context <job/startup/client-context.json> --month-intel <skill/references/sa-context-2026-11.json> --strategy <fresh-exact-Dynamics-envelope.json> --skills <repo/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/skills> --output <private-job/preproduction>`

Read its `PLAN-FROM-HERE.md`, then put the completed `foundation` into the
existing `STORY-PLAN.json`. Fields are validated by `edit_foundation.validate`:
dated country/month research and opportunity window; exact approved baseline
and assets; honest Dynamics strategy authority; source-bound0–3s visible beats
inside the EXISTING chosen `hooks` record (`kind`, `source_binding`, `beats`);
all five craft decisions; same selected visual IDs; baseline-bound design.
`FOUNDATION-01` in the loaded owner register requires this for new production.
Old approved outputs are not reopened or relabelled failed retroactively.
Structural checks cannot guarantee taste, a good hook, heard sound or retention.

## Useful starting choices, not templates

| Story/brand | Candidate existing techniques | Avoid/reject when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate/adviser explainer | stateful-ui-motion, staggered-type, planar-3d-camera, restrained scene-perspective-type; brand-voice-overlay only with cleared voice | Coded dashboards, floating generic finance icons, anonymous stock, unverified advice or poster opener replace people/proof |
| Luxury/product comparison | registered-slanted-panels, stacked-reaction-panels, kinetic-caption-finishing, restrained metallic-type-material | Flashes obscure dials/product finish; generic serif, fake ticking or repeated originals weaken confidence |
| Sport/action | motion-matched-speed, matched-flash-cut, bounded color-invert-glitch, source-led split screens, sound-led-cuts | Actual contact/action is lost, result/continuity invented, source blur retained or music feels childlike |
| Vehicle/service proof | grounded-freeze-frame, meaningful UI annotations, context-zoom-out, moving-panel-release | Fake before/after, random dashboard widgets or unrelated automotive sounds imply unfilmed work |
| Home/outdoor/food | real human/detail action, scene-perspective-type, subject-band-resolve, three-distinct-shot stacks where appropriate | Template overlays hide product, seasonal stock is misleading, effects replace the useful lived-in story |

These are starting shortlists. Current client identity/feedback/source/goal takes
precedence; read the actual card before selecting it.53 documented cards are not
53 mastered skills. Publish improvements in the same PR910/shared library;
MSI can retrieve them there, but its local installation needs actual verification.
