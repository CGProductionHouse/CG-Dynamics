# Hooks are an experience, not a compulsory heading

Use for opening development across clients. The3November owner correction explicitly includes visual, camera, sound, cutting, text and combined hooks. The user's illustrative dancer/breath/pause was not a requested script. Do not default to three headings when asked for three opening concepts.

A hook gives this viewer a reason to continue: a physical event, unresolved outcome, striking perspective, emotion, useful discovery or compelling proposition. Its payoff must belong to the client's story. An attention-grabbing effect can still fail if it hides the action, promises a false result or attracts the wrong audience. No template, cut rate or duration guarantees retention/conversion.

These are practical construction choices, not an exhaustive or universal taxonomy:

| Mechanism | Picture/time construction | Sound job | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decisive action | Enter on a readable peak; retain enough setup and consequence | Preserve the real event; music/accent supports it | Showing only an obscured contact or inventing its result |
| Anticipation | A loaded gesture, approach or imminent reveal earns a later outcome | Breathing/space, controlled rise or musical pocket | Holding too long without a meaningful question/payoff |
| Intimate human detail | Expression, sweat, hands, preparation; purposeful change of scale | Human effort/room/voice where genuinely present | Anonymous slow footage with no emotional or client connection |
| Camera reveal | Occluder passes, perspective changes or camera exposes meaningful space | Movement-led transition and a settled arrival | Digital zoom pretending to be an impossible physical camera move |
| Sensory/sound-first | An identifiable audible action leads its picture/context | Source event or intelligible voice is foreground | Generic boom over a picture it cannot plausibly belong to |
| Burst then reset | Compressed motifs/effects resolve into a readable scene | Short rhythmic construction opens into a pocket or phrase | A continuous flash montage with no orientation or story |
| Graphic transformation | Panels, depth, typography or material transforms into useful picture | Accents follow visible transformation, not every layer by default | Installed effects demonstrated without anything worth revealing |
| Match/pattern | Register a gesture, object, shape or direction across related shots | Phrase/accent connects the change | Similar-looking frames falsely presented as one continuous play |
| Contrast/reversal | Expectation changes through picture, context, scale or sound | Deliberate change of density/timbre, then resolution | Random inversion/volume jump mistaken for an idea |
| Proof/result first | A credible result opens a question about how/why | Real reaction or source action supports the proof | Unverified score, product benefit or misleading before/after |
| Viewer/POV challenge | Put the viewer in a specific decision or perspective | Relevant direct speech or action sound | Generic motivational challenge unrelated to the buyer |
| Text proposition | An interesting, specific statement/question reveals with intent | Reading space; reveal accent if appropriate | Empty slogan or type that simply names the obvious shot |
| Loop/open action | Begin within an action whose development or return rewards viewing | Phrase continuity and an earned return | Concealing a weak story through artificial replay |

Combine mechanisms only when they reinforce one idea. Text can be the primary hook; it is not the only form and should not be banned because earlier wording failed. Sound design is part of the construction from the first scene, not a loudness pass at the end.

## Use in the existing editing workflow

Before the full film, bind the opening to actual moving selects: first image/event → what changes → what remains unanswered → graphics/camera relationship → real/added sound → payoff → why this client. Read [sports opening construction](sports-opening-construction.md) for the actual Bloem/NBA/league study and [the owner's three new examples](../../../../scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/SPORTS-REFERENCES-20261103.md) for current evidence. Reuse the existing technique card/recipe that fits; a blank skill list or a number of effects is not a reason to render.

Compare clean and designed versions with the same source/time map; isolate sound changes using identical encoded picture. Inspect all effect frames at full and phone scale, then motion and exact sound when legitimate capabilities permit. Cut/attack measurements locate relationships, not identify beats or sound effects by themselves. Lyrics/ASR, mood tags and waveform peaks cannot establish a complete heard sound inventory.

Carry the selected mechanism into the full film's story and brand, with meaningful variation beyond the first title. Retain upright framing, appropriate stabilisation, matched exposure/colour, readable action and approved typography. Client-posted/source-history checks still apply. Record actual review limits; the older Fight Night preparation did not establish critically heard reference sound or mastered sports editing.
