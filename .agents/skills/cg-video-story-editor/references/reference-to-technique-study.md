## Transfer method

A highlight should reveal a bright duplicate through stationary glyph alpha, not move the letters with the mask. The [native caption study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-NATIVE-SHINE-STUDY-20261029.md) rejects a hard stripe and retunes feathered crop width/opacity; feather changes timing and highlighted area too. Check that reset actually removes animation, and separate source-alpha confinement from small encoded spill/parity failures.

Distinguish the one named control intentionally changed from every field the application actually saves. The [native RGB pulse study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-NATIVE-LENS-STUDY-20261029.md) retains an additional undocumented parameter change and a failed zero-strength encoded identity check. Native reopen parity does not clear either gate, and a chromatic depth split is not proof of geometric lens distortion.

After moving or inserting a Bézier key, inspect the protected interval before it: equal endpoint values can still bow when an incoming tangent survives. The [native inset study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-NATIVE-INSET-STUDY-20261029.md) retains the shrink failure and native tangent repair. Integer-rounded rectangle bounds can also misidentify adjacent source frames during fractional scaling; preserve that failed matcher and verify actual sampling geometry before claiming a time repeat.

A successful native timing test can still fail the source/story test. The [native inversion study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-NATIVE-INVERT-STUDY-20261029.md) verifies exact pulse frames and a protected revision while rejecting pale inverted coffee/large brightness jumps. Decode actual streams: a silent source can export an unwanted zero-valued AAC track. Reopen parity, neutral-effect parity, protected revision and creative fit are separate findings.

A layered source asset may remain editable in its authoring app while the editor imports a rasterized result. Record both sides separately and test actual save/close/reopen/export. The [native metallic study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-NATIVE-METAL-STUDY-20261029.md) retains Photoshop text/styles but merged Premiere PSD imports; the removed Bevel Alpha effect is not restored. Compare flat readability before accepting dimensional material, and preserve even a one-level reopen parity failure.

For a filmed display with a notch, fit the outer surface separately from the visible-content alpha. A concave notch can pull a fitted edge inward; a broad colour key can also catch unrelated scene pixels. The [full-shot phone study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-PHONE-SURFACE-STUDY-20261028.md) tests connected-region isolation and full-span controls. ## Match the technique to the film

- **Premium product or space:** reveal touch, material and the lived result; follow screen direction and spatial logic. A restrained cut, one movement match and quiet tactile foley may outperform a flash/glitch sequence. Give the detail and final space enough time to register.
- **Professional service:** lead with the buyer's decision or friction, then show a credible person working or listening and the specific help. Prefer natural performance, a precise quote or action and measured music. A finance or medical claim needs verified source text; effects cannot stand in for proof.
- **Event, sport or kinetic process:** rhythm, camera direction and sound transients can carry an opening; use ramps on action and reset the pace at a meaningful result. Do not keep the whole film at teaser speed.
- **Testimonial/interview:** the best spoken phrase is the hook. Use transcript tools for discovery, then hand-pick expression and breath. J/L cuts and room tone make a cut feel lived-in; decorative transitions are rarely the main value.
- **Retail or food:** show the product in use, not merely a beauty shot. A repeatable hand action can support a flash match cut; close sound can create appetite if it matches the photographed action.

Before a content run, translate the chosen mechanism into a shoot request: the decisive action in close, medium and result views; a few seconds before and after the action; two intentional screen directions if a transition is planned; clean room tone and isolated real sounds; and a shot that can hold after the hook. This gives Franco and Sydney options rather than forcing a template.

## Practice and promotion gate

Current status: **44 indexed and visually sampled; 0 independently heard; 0 reproduced in a Premiere practice sequence; 0 staff-reviewed**. This is a research pass and a plan for deliberate practice, not a claim of mastery. The next study pass needs full audio/video listening on the most relevant references, timecoded analysis and replication. Do not promote a visually sampled idea into the production playbook until it works in a CG edit.

Use a short, rights-cleared practice timeline before a client deadline to try four contrasts:

1. **Rush and release:** six to ten distinct product/process actions, continuous riser, a meaningful held reveal, then a separate music entry. Export a clean-cut comparison. Verify the score never starts, stops and restarts to manufacture the pause; listen to both tails and the last five seconds.
2. **Motion relationship:** a clean action cut, a screen-direction match, and one masked flash on the same two shots. Choose by product legibility and whether the movement lands naturally. Inspect mask edges at 100% and the full frame at phone size.
3. **Type in the scene:** build one meaningful word behind a real foreground gesture, then compare a plain title. Keep native graphic and mask controls or a documented fallback for another editor.
4. **Sound detail:** separate music, production sound, room tone, foley, transition accents and brand sound. Audit J/L overlap and audible tails, then remove each decorative cue in turn; retain only cues that make an action or story turn clearer.

For each test, log source fps, exact in/out and keyframes, build minutes, render issues, licence, clean fallback and second-editor reopen. Ask Franco, Sydney or the relevant editor to watch the two versions at phone size and note the *specific* moment they prefer and why. A method becomes a default option only after it works on real client footage, survives a Premiere handoff, and its effect on the buyer's understanding is supported by review. Performance claims require actual post metrics after publication; high view counts on a tutorial do not establish transfer to CG clients.

The28 October negative/band/film follow-up demonstrates separate layer hypotheses: global inversion changes identity colours; masked background inversion still creates a large brightness jump; RGB/alpha bands must protect unintended face/crop damage; a rounded card/glow does not prove film transport, and too few shutter samples make ghost copies. Use the relevant cards/negative examples in the shared progress list; do not convert these source-specific outcomes into a mandatory client look.

## Natural plane tracking needs spatial support and material judgement

Use a plain source and fixed camera-space title control before claiming attachment. Low held-out feature error can conceal inliers clustered in a narrow strip; log spatial spread and extrapolation and independently compare rectified natural texture. The [office wood study](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/blob/codex/nolan-office-research-874/scripts/video-editing/studio-phase2/OFFICE-UNKEYED-PLANE-STUDY-20261028.md) refreshes features on the actual wood, excluding raised/reflective objects.
