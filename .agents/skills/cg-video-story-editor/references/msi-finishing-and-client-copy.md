## Direct client corrections are executable inputs

Before a new edit or revision, read the client's current direct corrections and
latest authorised feedback; save literal copy restrictions in that exact client's
existing `OWNER-DECISIONS.json`. Do not turn a client naming requirement into a
global restriction on unrelated clients. TBS Brokers' client correction of
27 October, repeated by CA on 5 November, prohibits authored `TBS` alone. The
MSI R03 nevertheless contained `ASK TBS`: documentation without an active check
failed. All authored brand-name copy must use `TBS Brokers`; this is a naming
instruction, not an independently established legal conclusion.

Use `copy_constraints` in the existing decision file, for example:

```json
{"copy_constraints":[{"id":"full-brand-name","kind":"full_name","short":"TBS","full":"TBS Brokers","evidence":"Direct client correction 27 October; owner repeated 5 November; retain exact message reference in the client kit"}]}
```

`forbidden_phrase` with a literal `phrase` handles a client-prohibited offer or
wording. No arbitrary regular expressions. Keep the actual feedback message/date
and supersession state in the same client record. Do not invent restrictions
from examples. When the loaded rules include `COPY-02`, the existing story plan
must contain `authored_copy` entries for picture, cover and caption:

```json
{"authored_copy":[{"surface":"picture","text":"ASK TBS BROKERS\nABOUT YOUR COVER","source":"Exact selected scene/layer/artwork path"},{"surface":"cover","text":"","source":"Actual text-free cover composition"},{"surface":"caption","text":"Exact proposed caption","source":"Actual caption file"}]}
```

Inventory the **selected** layers and nested/pre-rendered artwork, not a rejected
renderer version. Keep a designed multiline phrase in one entry so line breaks
do not split its full name. Preflight checks the inventory against the verified
loaded client decisions; release repeats that check and reads the actual caption
file. Re-run after any text/caption change. These are text checks, not OCR or proof
that every pixel was inventoried: inspect every encoded title, cover, logo and
embedded text too. The job cannot claim restrictions loaded until its real client
kit/snapshot was reconciled. A fixture passing is not a repaired client film.

## Finishing choices inherited by the next film

- **Typography:** keep multiline phrases compact with optically checked leading
  and common baselines. WiseRide's owner rejected hard black borders/offset hard
  shadows; use a restrained soft shadow when contrast needs it, unless the exact
  brand calls for another treatment. Animate hierarchy/words with purpose while
  preserving reading holds. A generic underline and convenient font are not a
  designed brand treatment. Use verified current Canva/Adobe/client assets.
- **Picture:** record the stabilisation decision on every ordinary/focused shot:
  applied parameters and comparison, witnessed already-stable original, or a
  justified intentional-motion exception. Preserve smooth captured moves and
  match compatible directions across cuts. Correct each recording's orientation
  first. Do not infer camera shake from a moving arm/hose feature track.
- **Grade:** correct exposure and white balance per shot, match neighbours, then
  style. WiseRide R03 used fixed shot-specific corrections to avoid alternating
  yellow/washed-out bodywork; these values are source-specific. A dark filter or
  temporal auto-exposure is not a shortcut. Recheck faces, whites, product colour,
  transitions and the exact cover after export; clipped source detail stays lost.
- **People/mattes:** inspect the source camera frame for truncated limbs before
  masking. Test edges on light and dark plates at full size through all poses;
  frame the clean upper body to leave at the delivery boundary or retain the
  intact filmed shot if the cutout floats/fringes/crops an arm. Never fabricate a
  limb or call one clean still a passed moving matte. Muted B-roll requires a
  moving mouth check across the entire selected interval to exclude speech.
- **Sound:** anticipate an action when useful, play the cue through its visible
  action, and let a controlled tail cross the following cut. EHP's sizzle stopped
  and restarted without its action: reject that envelope. No arbitrary fire/SFX
  inserts, automatic whoosh-per-cut or louder-all-cues repair. Keep useful genuine
  cheers/action under the music and crew direction out. Listen to stems and the
  exact mix using the qualified shared route; measurements alone are not hearing.
- **Shot and ending purpose:** when asked for a moving shop/people ending, another
  raw-product close-up does not satisfy it. Keep the accepted music/story while
  replacing the rejected action. Use the exact client's official approved logo
  treatment; WiseRide's clean Group reveal is client-specific, not a new universal
  shortened-outro rule. Keep music continuous and logo source audio muted.
- **Cover:** choose a strong correctly oriented, corrected client-relevant image
  and the real supplied logo variant. No mandatory headline, fabricated recolour
  or white logo tile. Check full portrait and actual small centred grid crops.
