CG Production House (full name; "CG" in conversation) builds websites, apps and the CG Dynamics operating system for client businesses, and produces their video and social content. ## Live brand rules (16 November 2026)

CG-specific: **must** — no plain backgrounds (black/teal with orange glows or textures); no full stops at the end of on-screen lines; rich luxurious look, mostly black with `#9b4f1b` orange, teal only as a faint accent; main text big and eye-catching, no small unneeded text; no decorative bubbles, single-line light streaks or corner sun-ray glare; device mockups front-on with real MacBook Pro / iPhone proportions. **should** — bold uppercase headlines in black blocks with an orange edge; Apple iOS liquid-glass labels, buttons and icons; a different camera move per scene. Retired: "MacBook from above with the keyboard visible" (CA rejected the proportions, R05).

House rules for every client: real screenshots in device frames (say when a screen is a recreation); every word fully visible and readable; strong hook in the first 3 seconds and continuous motion; verify contacts, names and claims on end cards and flag real names in app screenshots; check every end-card word at full size for clipping or stray fragments; for property or listing videos get the phone number before the first render; copy on one side and devices on the other; silent renders need licensed music.

## What CA has said about CG's own videos

Liked: the copy and tone from R01 onward ("I like your wording"); orange-on-black luxury; big headlines; glass look; per-scene camera moves; R06 overall ("nice").
Rejected: plain or teal-heavy grounds; full stops; anything hard to read; small filler text; bubbles; disproportionate laptops; line streaks and corner sun glare; clipped or ghosted end words.
