# AI Workforce — Industry Research Index

Last updated: 2026-07-26
Status: Canonical industry-library research programme.

## Standard

Industry libraries are built from authoritative evidence before client classification.

Each industry pack must contain:

- industry scope and sub-segments;
- official South African economic and regulatory sources;
- market structure and buyer journey;
- terminology;
- typical products/services and decision drivers;
- claims and compliance risks;
- organic and paid content implications;
- website and lead-funnel implications;
- measurable outcomes;
- source-by-source findings and limitations;
- review-gated candidate Skill Cards;
- client-classification research prompts;
- remaining unanswered research questions.

Client-specific retrieval remains separate:

```text
Master Marketing Library
→ Industry Library
→ exact active client knowledge
→ current platform/channel knowledge
→ cited output
```

A client is never classified from its name, an industry guess or this research index.

## Per-industry completion rule

An industry is not marked ready because a document exists.

It requires:

- at least five strong authoritative sources where available;
- South African regulatory coverage;
- at least one official economic/statistical source where applicable;
- buyer and lead journeys;
- claim-risk register;
- platform/content application;
- reporting and outcome model;
- candidate Skill Cards with limitations;
- human review state;
- no client knowledge leakage.

## Source hierarchy

1. South African legislation, regulators and official statistics.
2. Official industry bodies and professional councils.
3. Primary international authorities where local material is insufficient.
4. Peer-reviewed/open-access research.
5. Lawfully available public-domain books and archives.
6. Verified client evidence.
7. CG performance observations, kept client-specific unless independently validated.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No name-only industry classification.
- No unsupported market-size or growth figure.
- No legal, financial, medical or professional advice.
- No client claim promoted to industry truth.
- No one client's performance generalised universally.
- No copyrighted text ingestion without rights evidence.
- No unreviewed industry knowledge in normal agent answers.
