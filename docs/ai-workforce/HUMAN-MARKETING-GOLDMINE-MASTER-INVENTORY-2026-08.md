## Registration unit required per source

Each source referenced across the packs must become its own record where appropriate, carrying:

- title;
- source organisation or author;
- source type;
- publication or update date;
- access date;
- canonical URL or repository path;
- rights and licence state;
- jurisdiction;
- industry and channel tags;
- source summary;
- extracted findings;
- limitations;
- prohibited overclaims;
- review state;
- reviewer and review date;
- freshness cadence;
- citation granularity;
- replacement or supersession link.

A whole goldmine document must not be registered as one flattened source when it contains multiple independent sources with different rights, dates, strengths and limitations.

## Knowledge conversion rule

Source records remain separate from company knowledge.

Only evidence-supported findings may become knowledge candidates. Every derived knowledge item or skill card must:

- cite its source records;
- preserve limitations and uncertainty;
- stay in draft or review until human approval;
- remain excluded from normal production answers until approved;
- avoid turning one client observation into industry truth;
- avoid turning agency-reported performance into universal causality.
