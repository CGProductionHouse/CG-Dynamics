# Industry Marketing Activation Retrofit Tracker

Last updated: 27 July 2026

## Purpose

Track the structured retrofit of the existing CG Dynamics industry libraries with the stronger marketing-activation layer defined in `AI-MARKETING-RESEARCH-IMPLEMENTATION-READINESS.md`.

This is research-library work first. Implementation into production Skill Cards, agents or client strategy outputs happens only after the source-backed packs are reviewed and converted into `needs_review` candidates.

## Required activation layer

Every applicable industry should ultimately include:

- buyer pains;
- buying triggers;
- audience roles;
- ad angles;
- hook directions;
- proof assets;
- offers;
- lead magnets;
- video and content formats;
- landing-page structure;
- objection handling;
- retargeting stages;
- meaningful performance metrics;
- claims, rights and compliance boundaries;
- source register and review backlog.

## Conversion after research

After all retrofit batches are researched:

1. audit against existing industry packs and Skill Cards;
2. remove duplicates and conflicts;
3. create narrow candidate Skill Cards only;
4. default every candidate to `needs_review`;
5. link exact source records, limitations and prohibited overclaims;
6. review for rights, freshness and jurisdiction;
7. test representative client scenarios without hard-coded clients;
8. activate only explicitly approved cards;
9. verify normal staff retrieval cannot see research-only or needs-review knowledge.
