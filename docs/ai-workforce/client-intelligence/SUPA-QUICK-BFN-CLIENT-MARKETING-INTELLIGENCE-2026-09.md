## 6. Human marketing voice

Supa Quick BFN should sound like a **real Bloemfontein fitment/workshop team talking to real motorists**.

### Use

- practical, specific language tied to the actual service or driver situation;
- a local, approachable tone without forced local slang;
- short, clean sentences;
- confident competence shown through what is happening, not self-praise;
- useful explanations of what a motorist might notice or need to check;
- natural `we`, `our` and `us` when the team or workshop is genuinely the subject;
- natural English by default; everyday Afrikaans when requested or clearly appropriate;
- light humour when the real workshop/staff moment gives it a reason to exist;
- interactive ideas that invite a real guess, choice or observation.

### Avoid

Generic automotive filler, including:

- `drive with confidence`;
- `your trusted tyre partner`;
- `keep your journey moving`;
- `quality tyres at great prices`;
- `where quality meets service`;
- `experience the difference`;
- generic `we've got you covered` copy;
- unsupported `best`, `safest`, `fastest`, `premium` or `expert` claims used as empty persuasion.

National Supa Quick slogans can appear when supplied in approved brand artwork, but should not become the default voice of every BFN caption.

## 7. Captions — durable BFN rules

**Captions add to the post or video. They do not repeat it.**

- Start from the exact image/video and what the artwork already says.
- If the artwork names the service, add the motorist problem, useful context, human observation or practical next step instead of paraphrasing the headline.
- Do not copy Reel dialogue into the caption.
- Keep straightforward service captions relatively short; a longer caption must earn its length with useful explanation.
- Avoid identical openings and recycled closing lines across consecutive posts.
- Do not reuse an exact text-on-post line after CG says it has already been used.
- When multiple assets are supplied, account for **every asset** and keep one caption/output aligned to each requested asset.
- Mention Bloemfontein naturally where it improves local discovery; do not force the city into every sentence.
- Use branch-specific contact details only when the branch is known or explicitly requested.
- For broad BFN creative where a website CTA is useful, `wisemangroup.co.za` is the established CG route.
- Do not force a sales CTA into a human/team post.
- If a price, special or competition appears in the supplied creative, use only the exact supplied current terms and dates.

### Caption pressure level

Useful first. Commercial when the post is commercial. Avoid shouting, false urgency and repetitive `book now`/`visit us today` endings. A tyre-shop caption should help the motorist understand why the post matters.
