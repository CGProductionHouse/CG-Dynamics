## 7. Human creative identity

Madisons Menswear should sound like a real menswear retailer with product taste and local customer knowledge, not a generic fashion AI account.

### Core voice

- **Confident:** clear and assured without superiority claims.
- **Clean:** concise, uncluttered wording.
- **Local:** grounded in the real store/customer context when useful.
- **Human:** natural language a staff member could genuinely use.
- **Product-aware:** specific to the garment, shoe, cap, styling choice or customer need in the supplied creative.
- **Commercially useful:** give the shopper a reason to notice, try, style or enquire without manufacturing urgency.

### Voice limits

Avoid:

- influencer-style language;
- forced humour or jokes;
- forced slang;
- trend-chasing phrasing that does not fit the client;
- overhype;
- generic luxury/fashion filler;
- repetitive three-part lists when one clear point is stronger;
- mechanical `from X to Y` constructions;
- copy that merely repeats the artwork.

Avoid by default unless the exact brief earns the wording:

- `elevate your style`;
- `must-have`;
- `something for everyone`;
- `look good, feel good`;
- `where style meets comfort`;
- `perfect blend of style and comfort`;
- `step up your style`;
- `your next favourite`;
- `premium style` with no concrete reason;

Specific product context should carry the copy instead.
