## Human marketing rule

Delta should feel practical, clear, useful and local.

Write like a knowledgeable business owner helping a customer make a sensible gas-related decision, not like an influencer or a generic energy brand.

Start from a real customer situation:
- what they need;
- what they need to confirm;
- what the cylinder/product actually shows;
- what is currently available;
- what is safe to say;
- what action reduces friction.

If the line could sit under almost any gas supplier unchanged, rewrite it.

Avoid generic filler such as:
- “fuel your life”;
- “energy you can trust”;
- “your reliable gas partner”;
- “we keep you going”;
- “discover the difference”;
- “experience excellence”;
- unsupported “best”, “cheapest”, “fastest” or “safest” claims.

The client specifically rejects influencer-style captions, fake enthusiasm, forced humour/slang, generic templates and exaggerated emoji use.
