## Commercial reality

Central Canvas is not primarily an event-tent business and should not be positioned that way.

Its strongest defensible commercial territory is:

> Custom cargo-protection and heavy-duty cover solutions for transporters, farmers, fleet operators, businesses and private customers who need durable products, repairs and practical local service.

The business solves expensive operational problems:

- cargo exposed to rain, dust, wind and UV;
- unsafe or poorly secured loads;
- vehicle downtime caused by damaged tarps;
- fleet inconsistency;
- custom equipment or machinery that standard retail covers do not fit;
- costly replacement when repair is still viable;
- lack of a local supplier capable of measuring, manufacturing and repairing.

Marketing should sell reduced downtime, load protection, fit, repairability and reliability—not only fabric.

## Buyer journey

### Trigger

The buyer notices:

- a tear, worn seam, failed buckle or damaged eyelet;
- cargo being exposed;
- a new truck, trailer or machine needing a cover;
- an upcoming season or long-distance route;
- insurer, client or safety requirements;
- a standard cover that does not fit;
- rising cost of repeated low-quality replacements.

### Research

They compare:

- repair versus replacement;
- price;
- expected durability;
- material type and weight;
- turnaround time;
- fit and reinforcement;
- collection or delivery;
- ability to handle fleet volume;
- reputation and real work examples.

### Enquiry

They should be able to send:

- product type;
- dimensions;
- photos of the vehicle/equipment;
- photos of damage;
- usage environment;
- required date;
- location;
- number of units;
- whether they need repair, replacement or assessment.

### Conversion

Central Canvas should respond with:

- clear recommended solution;
- scope and exclusions;
- material and reinforcement description;
- quote validity;
- expected turnaround;
- collection/delivery method;
- warranty or workmanship terms where applicable;
- repairability guidance.

### After-sales

Request:

- installation or in-use photos;
- review focused on fit, turnaround and service;
- permission to feature the project;
- reminder for future inspection or replacement cycle;
- fleet or repeat-order discussion.
