## Stable Agri-Secure context

Agri-Secure is treated in the current client-specific research as a South African farm/rural security marketing client serving the context of farms, smallholdings, and rural agricultural businesses.

Use these durable positioning rules:

- Frame security as layered rural risk management, not as a single gadget or generic suburban alarm product.
- Prefer calm readiness, practical protection, operational competence, and clear risk reduction.
- Speak to rural operating realities such as distance, access points, perimeter scale, family/workforce safety, machinery, vehicles, fuel, livestock, equipment, night exposure, and isolation when relevant.
- Keep the tone direct, practical, rural-aware, competent, and restrained.
- Avoid fear theatre, sensational crime framing, militaristic supremacy language, unsupported absolutes, or generic urban-security clichés.
- Respect the dignity of farm owners, families, workers, managers, and communities; do not use trauma or political identity as a marketing shortcut.
- When conversion is the goal, prefer a consultation or site-specific assessment journey over a one-size-fits-all package narrative, unless the client provides a different current offer.

These are marketing/brand intelligence rules. They are not proof of a specific operational service, geographic footprint, credential, or response promise.

## Facts that are not yet safe to publish without verification

Do not infer or publish the following from sector research alone:

- exact services or hardware Agri-Secure supplies
- direct armed-response or monitoring capability
- partner-delivered response/monitoring arrangements
- service areas, towns, municipalities, or local-proximity claims
- response times or guaranteed arrival windows
- operating hours, 24/7 availability, or emergency process
- phone numbers, email addresses, website, social handles, or fixed footer details
- legal/trading-name details beyond the client name already used in approved research
- registration, licensing, accreditation, memberships, or compliance claims
- pricing, packages, promotions, contract terms, or guarantees
- connectivity, load-shedding, backup-power, integration, or specific technology claims
- proof assets, testimonials, partners, staff names, or image permissions

If a task needs any of these, retrieve current Agri-Secure evidence first. If live verification fails or evidence conflicts, preserve the stored value as unverified and omit or qualify the public-facing claim as required by the freshness protocol.

## Language and tone

The research supports English and Afrikaans as potentially strong audience-fit languages, but no permanent Agri-Secure language split was confirmed in the audited migration corpus.

Therefore:

- choose language from the current brief or confirmed audience context;
- when writing Afrikaans, write natural local Afrikaans rather than mechanically translating English;
- do not convert the research observation into a permanent bilingual posting rule unless the user confirms it.

## Dynamics retrieval: happy-day workflow

For every Agri-Secure task that depends on client-specific facts or preferences:

1. Resolve the active client as Agri-Secure and keep the client boundary explicit.
2. Retrieve relevant Agri-Secure Dynamics memory before relying on old chat history or generic model knowledge.
3. Layer the retrieved context:
   - stable identity / durable brand rules;
   - persistent confirmed preferences;
   - recent task/campaign context;
   - human-marketing/cultural intelligence from the existing research pack;
   - source/provenance metadata and freshness state.
4. Use only the layers required for the task; sensitive/recent operational context should not be surfaced merely because it exists.
5. For mutable facts required in public copy, verify freshness or preserve them as unverified according to the shared freshness protocol.
6. Draft from the strongest current evidence and suppress duplicate rules/research.
7. After a material user correction or new confirmed client fact, write it back to the correct Agri-Secure memory layer with provenance rather than only relying on chat history.

## Evidence hierarchy

When Agri-Secure sources disagree, prefer:

1. current explicit Agri-Secure user/client confirmation;
2. current authoritative live verification, when appropriate and available;
3. an approved Agri-Secure Project Source or client-authored source with clear provenance;
4. approved Agri-Secure Dynamics memory/research with evidence and date metadata;
5. older chat or campaign evidence;
6. generic knowledge only for non-client background, never as a substitute for Agri-Secure facts.

Unrelated-client content is never evidence for Agri-Secure.

## Failure and degraded behavior

If Agri-Secure retrieval is unavailable:

- do not silently substitute another client or generic security-client memory;
- use only facts supplied in the current conversation plus safe non-client background knowledge;
- state uncertainty when a required client fact cannot be established;
- fail closed on volatile public-facing facts such as contact details, service area, response promises, credentials, or active offers.

If live verification is unavailable:

- retain existing stored data as unverified where useful internally;
- do not present it as confirmed current truth;
- block a public claim when unresolved freshness/conflict materially affects it.

## Writeback and restart behavior

Write back durable Agri-Secure corrections when the user clearly establishes a reusable rule or verified client fact. Keep campaign-only instructions in recent/task context unless the user indicates they are permanent.

On a new chat or restart, Dynamics retrieval should reconstruct Agri-Secure context from the layered client memory and source inventory. Do not rely on the model remembering the migration conversation itself.

## Duplicate suppression

Before creating new durable Agri-Secure memory or research:

- retrieve existing Agri-Secure records;
- check the August 2026 intelligence pack and migration audit;
- update/supersede the existing record when evidence changes;
- avoid storing the same rule repeatedly just because it appears in multiple chats;
- keep campaign examples separate from confirmed persistent rules.

## Dynamic-client and zero-leakage behavior

The active client must be resolved dynamically. When Agri-Secure is active, only Agri-Secure client memory may supply client-specific facts and preferences.

Never copy contacts, services, slogans, offers, brand rules, tone conventions, footer formats, legal claims, creative lessons, or operational details from another CG client into Agri-Secure, even when the other client is in the same industry or uses similar terminology.
