## Purpose

This pack exists to help CG Dynamics and CG Production House make better marketing decisions for Bloem Action Sports across:

- league registrations and recurring participation;
- corporate events and team building;
- kids' parties and family bookings;
- venue hire and social events;
- brand recognition in Bloemfontein;
- repeat attendance and retention;
- reach, following, entertainment and engagement;

Client knowledge in this document must remain isolated to Action Sport. It may use the approved community/sport/entertainment industry library for support, but no Action Sport-specific observation becomes general industry truth.

## Executive view

Bloem Action Sports has a stronger commercial base than its digital presence currently communicates. The venue is not merely an indoor court facility. It has multiple recurring and once-off revenue engines:

1. structured indoor sports leagues;
2. junior and social participation;
3. corporate team building;
4. birthday parties;
5. private functions and venue hire;
6. bar, kiosk, coffee and spectator spend;
7. sponsorship and local business participation;
8. tournaments, finals and franchise pathway events.

The most important strategic opportunity is to reposition the venue from “a place where existing teams play” to “Bloemfontein's accessible indoor social-sport community”. The marketing should make it easy for people without a complete team, without strong fitness, or without prior experience to join.

The strongest defensible differentiators are:

- three indoor courts;
- all-weather play;
- multiple sports;
- structured graded leagues;
- qualified umpires;
- automatic fixtures and electronic scoring;
- team and individual statistics;
- family, spectator and social facilities;
- corporate and party formats;
- access to a national Action Sports system and tournament pathway.

## Current public reputation and customer language

A current Google-review aggregation reports approximately 4.4 stars from 163 reviews. This is strong local proof, although the exact live Google rating and review count must be checked directly before publication.

Recurring positive language includes:

- “awesome place to have some fun”;
- variety of sports;
- high-quality facilities and equipment;
- friendly and helpful team;
- children had loads of fun;
- family-friendly;
- lively atmosphere;
- good place to practise sport socially;
- drinks available for spectators;
- enjoyable for parents and children;
- good spirit and atmosphere.

Recurring friction or risk signals include:

- toilets situated away from the hall;
- concern about children walking to bathrooms without an adult;
- limited visible security in the surrounding area;
- confusion about how to join when a person does not already have a team;
- old public pricing or registration information still circulating on third-party sites;
- inconsistent descriptions of which sports are currently available.

### What the customer language tells us

The strongest emotional promise is not elite sport. It is:

- easy social competition;
- a place to belong during the week;
- fun with friends, colleagues or family;
- exercise that does not feel like a gym session;
- a safe structured game where the venue handles the admin;
- spectator enjoyment before and after the match;
- a party or corporate activity that does not require the organiser to build the programme.

Marketing should use this language instead of generic claims such as “feel the action” or “where champions are made”.

## Current digital and conversion assessment

### Website strengths

The local website does communicate:

- location;
- the three-court facility;
- core sports;
- parties, corporate events and venue hire;
- bar, kiosk, braai and screens;
- WhatsApp contact;
- the general all-weather benefit.

### Website weaknesses

The current site has several likely conversion problems:

1. The homepage repeats the same copy and uses broad claims rather than clear customer paths.
2. There is no strong first-choice split for:
   - join a league;
   - find a team;
   - enter a full team;
   - book a party;
   - book corporate team building;
   - hire the venue;
   - see fixtures or results.
3. The events calendar appears empty in indexed pages.
4. Pricing, league dates, available divisions and party-package detail are not clearly surfaced.
5. Action Soccer, Bubble Soccer and Action Rugby availability is inconsistent across local and national pages.
6. The website does not visibly exploit its review proof.
7. The copy does not strongly explain that beginners and individuals can join.
8. Page titles and search snippets are repetitive and weak.
9. There is no obvious downloadable corporate package or party package.
10. The website has conflicting operating-hour information compared with public listings.
11. There is no visible real-time content layer showing current leagues, finals, team stories or available spaces.
12. The visual presentation does not yet communicate a vibrant sports-and-social venue.

### Immediate website structure recommendation

The homepage should begin with four high-intent actions:

- Join a league
- Find me a team
- Book a kids' party
- Book team building or a function

Secondary actions:

- Fixtures and results
- Current leagues and available divisions
- Sports and rules
- Venue facilities
- Contact and directions

Every sport page should show:

- who it is for;
- team size;
- beginner suitability;
- season length;
- game frequency;
- typical game duration;
- current divisions;
- next start date;
- current price or “request current rate”;
- full-team and individual-entry routes;
- equipment supplied;
- exact call to action.

- three world-class outdoor padel courts;
- free introductory class;
- Playtomic booking;
- secure parking;
- pro shop;
- coffee shop;
- lockers, showers and jungle gym;
- rates from R75 per person per hour;
- sponsorship and event options;
- heritage-led club positioning.

Competitive strengths:

- extremely low-friction booking;
- a modern, fashionable sport;
- transparent entry pricing;
- free first experience;
- strong facility detail;
- clear beginner route;
- social-club positioning.

Action Sport response:

- publish clear entry products;
- offer a first social game or open-night mechanic;
- make “find a team” as easy as Playtomic court booking;
- market multi-sport variety and full structured league progression;
- show the after-game social atmosphere more strongly.

## Strategic position

Recommended positioning territory:

**Bloemfontein's organised indoor social-sport home — from first game to finals night.**

This territory balances:

- performance and competition;
- beginner accessibility;
- recurring community;
- after-work entertainment;
- family and corporate use;
- year-round all-weather reliability.

Do not position the venue only as:

- serious competitive sport;
- a kids' party venue;
- a bar attached to courts;
- generic fitness;
- a national franchise branch without local personality.

- sport;
- division;
- day;
- start date;
- season length;
- game frequency;
- price;
