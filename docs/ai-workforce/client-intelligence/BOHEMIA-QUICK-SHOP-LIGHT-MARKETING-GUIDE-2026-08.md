# Bohemia Quick Shop — Light Marketing Guide

Last updated: 2026-08-06
Status: Review-state client guidance. Keep isolated to Bohemia Quick Shop.
Scope: Two poster designs per month only.

## Identity

Client identity confirmed directly by CA as **Bohemia Quick Shop** through the supplied Facebook page.

The shared Facebook URL was not publicly crawlable in this research environment, so current products, specials, trading hours, location, contact details and active promotions must be taken from the client brief or checked manually on the page before every design.

## Marketing job

The purpose of the two monthly posters is not to build a full content ecosystem. Each poster must do one clear job:

1. create immediate local awareness of a timely offer, product or convenience benefit;
2. give nearby customers a simple reason to visit the shop now.

## Core audience

- nearby residents;
- commuters and passing traffic;
- workers and students needing quick convenience purchases;
- regular customers responding to familiar products, price and availability;
- people looking for snacks, drinks, household basics or other confirmed quick-shop items.

Do not advertise unconfirmed stock categories.

## Poster system

### Poster 1 — Sales or product focus

Use for:

- a confirmed special;
- a featured product or bundle;
- new stock;
- seasonal demand;
- payday or month-end value;
- cold-drink, snack, braai, grocery or convenience moments where confirmed.

Structure:

- one strong product or offer;
- large readable price where supplied;
- very short headline;
- exact validity period;
- address, contact or visit action;
- terms only where necessary.

### Poster 2 — Brand, occasion or convenience focus

Use for:

- weekend convenience;
- public holidays;
- school, work or travel routines;
- local event or rugby-weekend relevance;
- trading-hours reminders;
- a simple brand-recognition post when no strong special is available.

Structure:

- one relatable local moment;
- one clear reason Bohemia Quick Shop is useful;
- one visit action;
- minimal copy.

## Creative direction

- bold enough to read quickly on a phone;
- one dominant message, not a catalogue;
- use real product photography where possible;
- keep prices and key wording visually obvious;
- maintain consistent logo placement, type hierarchy and brand colours;
- avoid generic supermarket stock imagery when actual products can be photographed;
- avoid filler phrases such as “quality you can trust” or “something for everyone”.

The design should feel local, practical and energetic rather than corporate.

## Content angles

Useful recurring angles:

- “Sommer gou by Bohemia” convenience;
- weekend or braai essentials;
- cold-drink and snack moments;
- payday value;
- school-run or work-break convenience;
- rugby or local-event stock-up;
- new or limited stock;
- public-holiday trading reminders.

Language should follow the client’s actual audience and existing Facebook tone. Afrikaans-first is likely suitable for local Bloemfontein convenience retail, but confirm against current page usage.

## Competitor frame

The practical competitors are nearby convenience stores, forecourt shops, supermarkets and informal local retailers.

Bohemia Quick Shop should not try to beat supermarkets on range. The poster message should emphasise what a quick shop can credibly win on:

- proximity;
- speed;
- familiar local service;
- timely stock;
- simple specials;
- convenient opening hours, where confirmed.

## Monthly brief checklist

Before design starts, obtain:

- exact product or message;
- current price;
- offer dates;
- stock limitations;
- approved product photo;
- current address, contact and trading hours;
- whether Facebook-only or adapted to print/WhatsApp;
- required language;
- any supplier branding rules.

Never reuse an old price or promotion without confirmation.

## Measurement

For this small scope, keep measurement simple:

- poster reach and engagement;
- comments or inbox enquiries;
- customer mentions of the promotion;
- stock movement on featured products where the client can provide it;
- which offer type receives the strongest response.

## Recommended monthly rhythm

- **Poster 1:** direct product or special poster designed to drive visits and sales.
- **Poster 2:** timely convenience, occasion or brand-recognition poster.

This gives the limited two-poster package a balanced commercial purpose without pretending it is a full marketing programme.

## Guardrails

- Confirm every price, date and product before publishing.
- Do not invent product availability.
- Do not overload posters with multiple unrelated offers.
- Do not recommend paid campaigns, websites or large strategy work unless the client scope changes.
- Keep CG effort proportional to the actual two-poster monthly package.
