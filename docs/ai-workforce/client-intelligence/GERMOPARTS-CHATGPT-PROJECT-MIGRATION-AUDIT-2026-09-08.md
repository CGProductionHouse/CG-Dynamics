## 7. Human creative/caption conclusions

Germoparts content should:

- be human, practical and specific;
- feel product/application-aware rather than like generic automotive advertising;
- default to concise copy with short sentences;
- use one useful product/customer point instead of several broad claims;
- add to artwork/video rather than transcribe it;
- speak differently to workshops/trade and DIY owners where the context supports it;
- use technical detail only from current/supplied evidence;
- default to natural English unless the brief supports another language;
- use plain contact details without contact emojis;
- make branch identity explicit when the post is branch-specific;
- keep staff/event content warm without attaching an unrelated product promotion.

Explicit rejected/default filler includes:

- `keep your vehicle running`;
- `quality parts you can trust`;
- `performance you can rely on`;
- `your one-stop parts shop`;
- `we've got you covered`;
- `drive with confidence`;
- unsupported `quality/performance/reliability` stacks;
- captions that simply repeat the product card;
- unsupported specification/fitment dumps.

Normal caption rhythm supported by Project history:

- generally 2-4 short sentences before footer/hashtags;
- longer only where technical explanation genuinely helps;
- multiple options when requested, not as a mandatory default.
