## 6. Voice and human creative standard

Bohemia should sound like real local people running a useful Bloemfontein forecourt/shop, not a national convenience-chain ad generator.

### Tone

- **Confident and clear:** professional, straightforward, no inflated claims.
- **Clean and concise:** short enough to scan quickly.
- **Local and relatable:** Reddersburg Road / Bloem context when useful, without forcing locality into every line.
- **Real, not robotic:** simple natural language, real customer moments and actual services.
- **Professional:** truck/driver content should still feel polished and credible.

### Language

- Default to **natural English** for general social copy.
- A May 2026 coupon campaign was explicitly requested in English.

- Do not force bilingual copy, slang or stiff/formal Afrikaans.
- Prefer everyday wording that a normal customer understands. A durable correction is to use `drive` rather than niche/less-natural `haul` wording when speaking to drivers.

### Humour / playfulness

- Light warmth or situational wit is acceptable.
- Do not force humour, memes or TikTok-style trends.
- Do not use influencer-style hype.
- Do not overhype ordinary services.
- Never copy another quick-stop/fuel client’s voice.

## 7. Caption rules

Captions must **add to the artwork/video, not repeat it**.

For a normal poster or quick-stop shot, the caption should add one useful layer such as:
- the real customer moment;
- a practical reason the service matters;
- driver/truck-stop context;
- a useful detail not already on the artwork;
- the location/contact route;
- a current promotion condition;
- a simple next action.

Normal caption length: short to medium. Do not write a long paragraph around a simple forecourt or deli visual unless the brief genuinely needs explanation.

Strong openings are specific to what is happening. Examples of direction, not templates:
- a driver getting back on the road;
- the quick coffee/breakfast stop before work;
- diesel/refuelling without unnecessary delay;
- a practical Reddersburg Road stop during the day.

Avoid interchangeable filler such as:
- `your trusted stop`;
- `quality meets convenience`;
- `fuel your day`;
- `discover the difference`;
- `elevate your stop`;
- `something for everyone`;
- generic `reliable` claims with no concrete proof;
- generic chains of three service benefits merely to fill space.

Do not mechanically open every caption with `Quick stop`, `On the go`, `Back on the road` or another recurring phrase. Vary the angle around the actual creative.

### Contact rule

Normal marketing captions should include the confirmed footer/contact details. Do not substitute `DM us` for the phone/email route.
