## 5. Financial-services compliance and claim guardrails

Financial-services marketing is high-stakes. Clear, fair, specific and non-misleading wording is the baseline.

Never invent, infer or promise:
- investment returns or performance;
- guaranteed savings or premium reductions;
- guaranteed claim payment or approval;
- tax outcomes or tax savings;
- product suitability for a person/business;
- fees, rates, premiums or pricing;
- product eligibility or underwriting outcomes;
- cover limits, exclusions, excesses or waiting periods without current product evidence;
- FSP/licensing/representative status for the local office or an adviser;
- adviser authority beyond the current official title/evidence;
- insurer, underwriter or provider relationships;
- awards, rankings or accreditations;
- service turnaround times;
- exact product availability.

### Licensing/FSP rule

The current PSG site footer says affiliates of PSG Financial Services are authorised financial services providers. This is **national/group context only**. It does not establish the exact legal entity, FSP number, representative status or product authority of PSG Bloemfontein or a named adviser.

Do not publish an FSP number or `authorised/licensed for X` claim unless the exact local entity/adviser evidence is retrieved and current.

### Advice wording

Safe direction:
- encourage a conversation with the relevant adviser;
- explain what a customer may want to review or ask about;
- describe personal/commercial insurance advice at category level;
- use real-life risk context without fear-mongering;
- distinguish education from a personal recommendation.

Unsafe without exact supporting evidence:
- `this is the right cover for you`;
- `you will save`;
- `your claim will be paid`;
- `guaranteed protection`;
- unsupported comparisons or `best` claims;
- specific financial recommendations generated from generic audience assumptions.

Use the question as an educational hook, not as a pretext for an unsupported recommendation.
