## 9. Material claim guardrails

Material advice is product- and application-specific.

### Granite / natural stone

Natural variation matters. A sample or image does not guarantee identical slab pattern/colour. Do not call all granite stain-proof, maintenance-free or universally suitable for every application.

### Engineered quartz

Do not call quartz scratch-proof or heat-proof. Use current supplier care/performance documentation for the exact product.

### Porcelain / sintered surfaces

Current website categories include Infinity Porcelain and Stratus sintered porcelain. Thickness, edge handling, heat/stain performance and application suitability must come from the exact current supplier/product evidence.

### Marble

Market the natural appearance and veining without pretending marble behaves like every engineered surface. Etching, staining, sealing, patina and maintenance depend on the actual stone/use case.

Never invent exact performance limits, certifications, thicknesses, edge profiles, cut-out tolerances, outdoor suitability, sealing intervals or warranty terms.
