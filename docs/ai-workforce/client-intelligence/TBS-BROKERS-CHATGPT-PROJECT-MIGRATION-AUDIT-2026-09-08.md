## 6. Human creative/caption conclusions

Future TBS content should:
- sound like a real TBS person speaking to a client or local business owner;
- remain confident, clean, local and professional without becoming stiff;
- never become cheeky, influencer-like, trend-chasing or overhyped;
- add to the artwork/video rather than rephrase its visible wording;
- focus one post on one useful financial/risk decision, practical question, broker insight or real next step;
- identify the exact relevant cover/service when verified instead of hiding behind vague `solutions` language;
- explain practical relevance in plain language rather than fear or pressure;
- use natural Afrikaans or English/Afrikaans mixing only when it genuinely suits the audience and brief;
- keep emoji use restrained and functional rather than influencer-style;
- use client-service/team/community stories only when the underlying people, event and permission are real;
- use sponsorship/community content as human relationship content rather than forcing a financial-services sales pitch into every post;
- make the CTA fit the actual decision and current contact route rather than recycling one generic closer.

Rejected/overused directions include:
- `protect what matters`;
- `peace of mind` as filler;
- `your trusted broker` / `trusted partner` filler;
- `secure your future`;
- `tailored solutions for every need`;
- `insurance that matters` when it replaces a real point;
- fear-heavy `what if everything goes wrong?` hooks;
- generic `we are committed to...` corporate language;
- generic `we've got you covered` copy without a specific verified cover point;
- captions that simply restate poster headings/subtitles;
- permanent reuse of a fixed hashtag bank;
- treating a historical public caption as proof that its contact/footer is still current.

The official website itself contains some category-standard phrases such as `secure your future` and `peace of mind`. Under #248 these are factual/public evidence, not a future creative ceiling.

## 7. Financial/compliance/claim guardrails

TBS is a regulated financial-services client. Accuracy is the floor, not the finished marketing.

Never invent or infer:
- premiums, prices, fees or rates;
- investment returns, performance or forecasts;
- guarantees;
- tax outcomes;
- suitability or personalised recommendations;
- underwriting acceptance or eligibility;
- claim approval, payout or response outcomes;
- adviser authority beyond current exact evidence;
- licensing/FSP status beyond current authoritative proof;
- insurer/provider relationships;
- product/service availability for a particular person/business;
- service area or response time;
- awards, rankings or accreditations;
- testimonial outcomes.

When educational content discusses products, keep it clearly general unless the exact brief and compliance evidence support personalised advice. The official TBS disclaimer itself states that website information does not constitute personal financial advice and recommends personal advice before selecting a financial product/fund.

If FSP no. `47291`, adviser roles or another compliance credential is material to a public campaign, refresh the exact authoritative evidence at generation time rather than treating this migration date as permanent proof.
