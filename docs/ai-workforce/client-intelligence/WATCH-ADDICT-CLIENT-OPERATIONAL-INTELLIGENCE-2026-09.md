## 9. Human creative standard - Watch Addict mode

Watch Addict should feel premium and product-aware without slipping into generic luxury-watch AI filler.

### Core voice

- polished but human;
- confident without unsupported superiority;
- specific to the actual watch and wearer context;
- premium without sounding distant or pretentious;
- capable of bold/mechanical language for strong product visuals;
- capable of restrained emotional/lifestyle language for memory, occasion or gifting narratives when the creative earns it;
- commercially useful rather than decorative copy for its own sake.

### Caption behaviour

- start from the actual supplied watch/post/video;
- identify what the artwork already says;
- add a second layer instead of paraphrasing the artwork;
- use one concrete watch/customer/lifestyle angle rather than stacking generic adjectives;
- vary `watch` / `piece` / exact model or collection language naturally rather than repeating `timepiece` in every caption;
- keep technical details evidence-bound;
- do not force a spec dump into a lifestyle caption;
- do not force emotion into a technical/product-detail post.

### Avoid by default

Unless a specific creative concept genuinely earns them, avoid generic filler such as:

- `timeless elegance`;
- `make every second count`;
- `luxury on your wrist`;
- `elevate your style`;
- `the perfect timepiece`;
- `where style meets precision`;
- `more than just a watch`;
- `crafted for every moment`;
- `statement on your wrist` when it adds no specific idea;
- unsupported `premium quality`, `superior craftsmanship`, `investment piece`, `collector's item`, `limited`, `rare`, `exclusive` or `iconic` claims.

### Historical creative seeds

Bold directions like `PRECISION, REWIRED`, `ENGINEERED TO STAND APART`, `BOLD BY DESIGN` and `A DIFFERENT KIND OF LUXURY` can inform rhythm/attitude, but should not become a recycled slogan bank.

### Language
