## Human creative and caption rules

We Ar Fuels should sound like real people inside a Free State fuel-delivery business: **confident, clean, local, practical and professional**.

- no influencer-style language, forced humour, slang or overhype;
- use natural English or Afrikaans as the brief fits;
- captions add to the artwork/video rather than repeat it;
- stay concise and direct unless explanation is genuinely useful;
- anchor copy to the actual delivery, site, farm/business need, team, vehicle or customer context;
- prefer one clear idea per sentence instead of stacked generic lists;
