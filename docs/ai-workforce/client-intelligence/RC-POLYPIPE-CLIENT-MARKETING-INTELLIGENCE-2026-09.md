## Exact business and product scope

RC-Polypipe is an agriculture/irrigation client focused on flexible field-irrigation Poly-Pipe for South African farmers. The current official site positions the product as a low-pressure irrigation approach intended to distribute water along fields/headlands. This is not evidence that RC-Polypipe is a general civil, plumbing, construction or pressure-pipe supplier.

### Currently verified from the official site

- product material: **high-density polyethylene (HDPE) plastic**;
- material treatment/claim: **specially treated for UV resistance** and environmental durability;
- product form: flexible Poly-Pipe supplied by weight/roll-length relationship;
- use case: field irrigation on level or slightly downgraded headlands/farmland;

- the site says a minimum waterhead of about **1.8 m** is required for smooth entry/distribution on level or slightly downgraded headlands;

## Application and installation guardrails

The current official installation guidance supports these exact application facts:

- best suited to level-grade farms/headlands or slight downgrade;
- downgrade beyond roughly **0.61–0.91 m per 305 m** may require intermittent restrictions to maintain even flow;
- discharge pipe diameter should be equal to or smaller than the RC Poly-Pipe being attached;
- rough/sharp connection edges should be smoothed or taped;
- about **0.3 m / 30 cm** of Poly-Pipe is folded back to create a double layer at the connection;
- connection should be supported at ground level and left with slack;
- the roll can be pulled/unrolled across the headland with people or machinery as shown in the site guidance;
- trench guidance: continuous trench about half the pipe diameter deep and about the pipe diameter wide;
- 90-degree turns are described with about **1.8–2.4 m radius**;
- site guidance suggests a shovel of dirt every **4.5–6.1 m** to hold the pipe during installation;
- the open discharge end is positioned over a berm of about **1 m** for irrigation in the published guidance;
- the site warns against tying the pipe end because pressure can build up;
- venting is required; the site describes an open-top vent about **3 m** from the water source and additional vents at higher points;
- long runs may use published pressure-relief openings of about **1 cm × 1 cm**;
- water flow should begin low and increase gradually;
- gate holes/adjustable gates are made/installed around the **2–3 o'clock** position while filling.

The official site explicitly says these instructions are general guidance and that field/environmental conditions can require adjustment. Preserve that qualification in technical education.
