## 7. What the marketing should feel like

Supa Quick Centurion content should feel like it was written by someone who understands the real motorist decision in front of them.

Prefer:

- the actual tyre/fitment problem the customer notices;
- a practical reason a service matters;
- a useful next step;
- the specific vehicle/part/service visible in the creative;
- branch-local Centurion context when useful;
- simple explanations that help a driver understand what is being checked or fitted;
- human workshop detail when the creative shows the team/process.

Avoid generic tyre-shop filler such as:

- “drive with confidence”;
- “your trusted tyre partner”;
- “keep your journey moving”;
- “quality tyres at great prices”;
- “more than just tyres” used as empty filler;
- interchangeable “road ahead” / “peace of mind” slogans without a real point;
- vague claims of expertise, safety, quality or value that are not earned by the actual post.

Correct facts are the floor. The copy must still be worth reading.

## 8. Durable Centurion Project creative rules

Repeated accessible Centurion requests establish these durable rules:

### Captions

- **Do not repeat what is already written on the post.** Add context, a reason to care, a service insight or the next useful action.
- Keep the wording human and specific to the actual creative.
- Avoid generic automotive slogans and empty “expert” language.
- Do not copy text-on-post into the caption in slightly different words.
- Contact/footer details should be correct and branch-specific.
- Where SEO hashtags are requested, use focused exact-topic/local tags rather than a permanent bank.

### Text-on-post

- The user repeatedly asks for **non-generic**, **fresh** wording.
- Service posts should say something meaningful about the **service actually being offered**, not broad motivation or generic road language.
- Short wording is preferred when it still makes sense.
- Vary phrasing rather than repeating the same headline structure across consecutive posts.
- The visual should be able to carry the line quickly; do not overload it with caption-length copy.

### Tone

Default tone:

- direct;
- practical;
- confident without hype;
- local and human;
- service-aware;
- helpful to motorists.

Humour can be used where the creative supports it, but it should come from a real driver/workshop observation, not a forced meme voice.

## 9. Caption construction rule

A strong Centurion caption usually does three things:

1. notices something concrete in the image/video or service situation;
2. adds one useful layer the artwork does not already say;
3. gives a clear branch-specific next step when action is appropriate.

Examples of useful value-add angles:

- what a driver might feel/notice before needing the service;
- why the fitment/check matters;
- what the technician is doing in the visual;
- what information to bring when asking for a tyre quote;
- why exact tyre size/vehicle info matters;
- what to confirm before buying/replacing;
- when to contact the branch.

Do not invent diagnosis. If the brief says only “wheel fitment”, do not claim the vehicle had vibration, uneven wear or a safety defect.
