## Legal-market reality

People rarely search for `a law firm` in the abstract. They search because a specific problem has become urgent or risky:

- employee/employer dispute;
- summons or demand;
- contract problem;
- property transaction or dispute;
- debt / non-payment;
- estate issue;
- business transaction;
- divorce/family problem;
- criminal matter;
- regulatory or public-sector dispute.

The strongest legal marketing therefore starts with the **problem and next step**, not the firm's biography.

## Legal-marketing compliance

The Legal Practice Council Code of Conduct applies to attorneys, advocates, candidate legal practitioners and juristic entities.

The Code's publicity provisions expressly cover written, pictorial, oral, electronic and social-media publicity.

Marketing must not:

- bring the profession into disrepute;
- be offensive or inappropriate;
- procure work already entrusted to another attorney in an improper manner;
- misrepresent the nature of services offered;
- make unsupported outcome guarantees;
- create misleading impressions about expertise, status or service.

Every future HMH campaign should therefore be reviewed against the current LPC Code of Conduct, not only general advertising standards.
