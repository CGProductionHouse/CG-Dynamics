- Sharp, human, specific and commercially useful.
- Confident without sounding desperate, corporate, inflated or self-congratulatory.

- Use Bloemfontein/South African context naturally when it adds relevance. Do not force local references into every line.
- Write like people who actually do the work, not like an agency-template generator.

### Caption behavior

- **Add, do not repeat.** If the poster/reel already says the offer/service/vote/message, the caption should add implication, personality, context, proof, tension, benefit or CTA rather than restate the artwork.
- Avoid opening with the service name merely to label the post. Lead with the audience/business idea where possible.
- Keep short social captions genuinely short when the brief asks for short copy; do not pad a simple visual with a paragraph of filler.
- Service/sales content should focus on one clear customer/business value and one useful next action.
- Culture/BTS content can be personal, playful and use “our”, “we” and “us” naturally; it should feel like the real team rather than a brand monologue.
- Event/location-specific creative can be conversational and situational. Temporary campaigns do not become permanent voice rules.
- English is publicly verified. Afrikaans or mixed-language copy may be used when the actual brief/audience/history supports it; do not freeze a bilingual pattern without evidence.
- Emojis are contextual, not mandatory. Use them to support scanability or tone, not as decoration.

### Hashtags

- Maximum **5** hashtags.
- Select dynamically from the actual topic, service, audience/location and campaign.
- Do not preserve or paste a frozen generic hashtag bank.
