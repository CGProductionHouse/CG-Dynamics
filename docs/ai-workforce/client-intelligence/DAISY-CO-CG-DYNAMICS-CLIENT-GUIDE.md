## 2. Working brand voice

Daisy & Co should feel warm, cheerful, local and genuinely human.

Copy should be:

- specific to the actual product, occasion or visual;
- easy to read;
- playful when the post earns it;
- tasteful without becoming generic lifestyle language;
- useful enough to help someone choose, visit or act.

Avoid generic AI filler such as:

- `treat yourself`;
- `something for everyone`;
- `your one-stop destination`;
- `elevate your space/look`;
- `discover the difference`;
- vague `make every moment special` copy;
- generic `South African favourite` lines with no real idea.

If feedback says the line is generic, change the creative angle rather than lightly rewording the same sentence.

## 3. Captions

**Captions add to the artwork/video; they do not repeat it.**

- Start from what the audience can already see/read.
- Add a customer situation, product detail, personality, reason to care, useful context or action.
- Keep ordinary social captions short when the artwork already carries the detail.
- Use longer copy only when the story needs it.
- Vary openings, rhythm and CTA.
- Do not mechanically list several generic benefits.
- Default to one strong finished answer unless the brief asks for multiple options.

## 4. Text-on-post

Prefer:

- a short heading;
- a short supporting line only when needed;
- clear meaning on first read;
- wording tied directly to the product or occasion;
- cleverness that still makes sense.

Do not hide the real product behind abstract lifestyle copy.

## 5. Language

- English and Afrikaans are both valid depending on the brief.
- Afrikaans must sound like natural everyday spoken Afrikaans, not stiff/formal translation language.
- Heritage Day correction: use `tradisies` naturally when referring to South African traditions rather than the stiffer `gebruike` wording previously rejected.
- Do not force language mixing for effect.

## 6. Seasonal / cultural lessons

### Heritage Day

Make it about South African people, cultures and traditions in a human, celebratory way. A colourful table/product visual can support the idea, but avoid empty rainbow-nation slogans or generic national-pride filler.

### Women's Day / Women's Month

Do not turn it into Mother's Day. Womanhood, sisterhood, friendship, work/life roles and flowers/gifting can all be valid depending on the creative.

### Spring

Keep it fresh, fun and slightly unexpected. Avoid a stock seasonal montage with generic `new season` wording.

### Closing-date / interactive posts

Make the action and date immediately understandable. Fun or witty is welcome; do not default to `last chance`.
