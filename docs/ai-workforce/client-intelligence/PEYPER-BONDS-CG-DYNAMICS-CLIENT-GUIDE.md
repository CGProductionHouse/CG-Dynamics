## 7. Caption rules

**Caption = new value, not a rewrite of the artwork/video.**

A good Peyper Bonds caption usually does one of these:

- answers the next question a buyer would ask;
- explains one step in normal language;
- adds a practical preparation tip;
- shows what the team does behind the scenes;
- gives an estate agent useful context;
- explains why comparing offers matters without promising a result;
- connects a current verified rate/property development to the buyer's process.

Keep the copy relatively concise. One clear point beats three generic benefits in one sentence.

Where a visual already contains the key figures, do not repeat all the figures in the caption unless they are needed for understanding.
