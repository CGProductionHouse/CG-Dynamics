## Candidate industry cards (all `needs_review`)

- Farmers buy on proof, not adjectives (buyer psychology)
- Demonstration and field days beat claims (sales motion)
- Sell to the season, not the calendar month (timing)
- Communicate total cost of ownership and risk reduction (commercial economics)
- After-sales support and parts availability are the real product (retention)
- Water and energy efficiency is a commercial argument in SA (value drivers)

Each carries evidence label, confidence, application, mistakes-to-avoid and agent
instructions, and is flagged `candidate_unverified` pending reviewer confirmation
against the authoritative sources above.

## Sub-libraries

- **RC Polypipe** → Agriculture → Irrigation & farm water → low-pressure field
  irrigation. See below.
- **CASE Bloemfontein** → Agriculture → Agricultural machinery → dealership &
  after-sales. See below.

### RC Polypipe (client-isolated, `needs_review`)

Classified from rcpolypipe.com: UV-treated HDPE **field-irrigation** pipe for SA
farmers; suitable for level/slightly-downgraded land; **cannot pump uphill**,
needs ~1.8 m minimum water head, pump ~150k–350k L/h. **Not** a general
civil/plumbing/construction account.

The **"94% more cost-effective"** figure is a **client-published marketing
claim**, stored as `client_opinion` with a verification request (compared system;
assumptions; field size; equipment/install/pumping/labour/maintenance costs;
lifespan; calculation period; source date). It is never presented as independent
proof.

### CASE Bloemfontein (client-isolated, `needs_review`)

Classified from casebloemfontein.co.za: **agricultural machinery dealership**
(Case IH primary: tractors, harvesters, planters, tillage, hay & forage,
application, precision, Northmec implements) with **secondary** CASE construction
machinery and Husqvarna outdoor power, plus a **parts division and workshop /
after-sales**. Marketing must support sales + parts + service + technical
expertise + retention — not product posters alone.
