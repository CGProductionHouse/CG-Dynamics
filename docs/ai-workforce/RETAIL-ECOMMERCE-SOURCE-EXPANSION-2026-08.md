### RE-X2 — Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002

Publisher: South African Government
Canonical page: https://www.gov.za/documents/electronic-communications-and-transactions-act
Source type: legislation
Rights state: official_reference
Jurisdiction: South Africa
Freshness: verify current consolidated amendments before legal activation

Verified scope:

- The Act regulates electronic communications and transactions.
- Ecommerce disclosures, electronic contracting and online transaction processes sit within a formal legal framework.

Safe marketing implications:

- Merchant identity, contact details, material terms, price and transaction information must be clear before commitment.
- Checkout design must not hide the nature or cost of the transaction.
- Online terms are operational requirements, not decorative footer copy.

Limitations:

- This is not case-specific legal advice.
- Exact consumer rights depend on the transaction and current law.

### RE-X3 — Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008

Publisher: South African Government
Canonical page: https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act
Source type: legislation
Rights state: official_reference
Jurisdiction: South Africa
Freshness: verify current consolidated text and applicable regulations

Safe candidate knowledge:

- Product descriptions, prices, promotions, delivery promises, returns, refunds and warranty claims must be truthful and supportable.
- Scarcity, discount and “free” claims require real evidence and complete conditions.
- A store policy cannot simply remove statutory consumer rights.

Prohibited overclaims:

- “No refunds under any circumstances.”
- “Guaranteed next-day delivery” without service-area, cut-off and fulfilment evidence.
- “Only one left” where the stock signal is not real.
- “Authorised reseller” without proof.

### RE-X4 — SARS VAT registration and vendor obligations

Publisher: South African Revenue Service
Canonical pages:

- https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/
- https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/register-for-vat/
- https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/value-added-tax/obligations-of-a-vat-vendor/

Source type: official tax authority guidance
Rights state: official_reference
Jurisdiction: South Africa
Freshness: critical

Verified findings:

- VAT status and invoice treatment must match the merchant's actual tax position.
- SARS states that non-resident suppliers of qualifying electronic services may have South African VAT obligations.
- VAT registration must never be inferred from a business name, website or product category.

Marketing implications:

- State whether prices include VAT only when verified.
- Separate product price, delivery, duties and taxes where applicable.
- Do not promise “no import fees” without evidence.

Limitations:

- Tax treatment requires authorised professional review.

### RE-X5 — Google Merchant Center Misrepresentation policy

Publisher: Google Merchant Center Help
Canonical page: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127
Source type: official platform policy
Rights state: metadata_and_link_only
Freshness: continuous
Policy clarification verified: October 2025

Verified findings:

Google treats the following as serious risks:

- false business identity or contact information;
- products the merchant cannot supply;
- false reseller, certification or brand relationships;
- non-delivery;
- unusable return or refund processes;
- undisclosed payment obligations;
- missing shipping, terms, returns or refund information;
- unavailable offers;
- deceptive or phishing destinations.

Operational implications:

- Website, feed, Merchant Center and ads must agree on identity, price, availability, shipping and returns.
- A written refund policy is not enough when the actual support process does not honour it.
- Egregious violations can cause immediate account suspension.

## New derived knowledge candidates — review state only

### Price and stock consistency

Rule:

- Feed, landing page and checkout must agree on price, currency, availability and variant.

Required evidence:

- catalogue source;
- last sync time;
- website check;
- checkout check;
- promotion dates;
- reviewer.

### Returns are operational truth

Rule:

- A return policy is not trustworthy unless the actual process can receive, assess and resolve a return according to the published terms.

Required evidence:

- policy URL;
- support path;
- return method;
- window;
- cost responsibility;
- refund/exchange outcome;
- latest operational test.

### Gross order is not net revenue

Rule:

Report separately:

- order created;
- payment authorised;
- paid;
- fulfilled;
- delivered;
- cancelled;
- returned;
- refunded;
- net retained value where verified.

### Promotion evidence

Rule:

Every sale, scarcity or deadline claim must retain:

- exact claim;
- product/variant scope;
- reference price;
- start and end time;
- stock evidence;
- conditions;
- reviewer;
- expiry state.

## Candidate Skill Cards — review state only

### RETAIL-CHECKOUT-TRUTH-AUDIT

Trigger: New store, checkout change or campaign launch.

Steps:

1. Verify legal merchant identity and contact information.
2. Confirm product, price, currency, stock and variant accuracy.
3. Confirm shipping cost, handling and transit time.
4. Confirm returns and refunds are visible and operational.
5. Complete a real test checkout without final payment where appropriate.
6. Confirm no hidden fees or misleading recurring commitment.
7. Record evidence, reviewer and date.

### RETAIL-MERCHANT-CENTER-SUSPENSION-TRIAGE

Trigger: Misrepresentation warning or suspension.

Steps:

1. Capture the exact policy reason and affected scope.
2. Compare business identity across website and Merchant Center.
3. Compare feed, landing page and checkout price/availability.
4. Verify shipping and returns policies and actual operations.
5. Remove false reseller, certification or scarcity claims.
6. Fix the source of truth rather than temporarily hiding products.
7. Request review only after the underlying issue is resolved.
8. Record outcome and recurrence risk.

### RETAIL-RETURN-POLICY-SYNC

Trigger: Return-policy change.

Steps:

1. Update the operational policy owner.
2. Update website policy.
3. Update Merchant Center account-level policy.
4. Update product-level overrides where needed.
5. Verify support scripts and staff process.
6. Test one representative return journey.
7. Record effective date and reviewer.
