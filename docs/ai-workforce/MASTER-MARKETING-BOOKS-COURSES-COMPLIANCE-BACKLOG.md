## Rights rule

A source being useful does not make it ingestible.

Use one of these modes:

- `public_domain_full_text` — full ingestion allowed when item-level rights evidence exists;
- `licensed_full_text` — ingest only under a licence that permits this use;
- `metadata_and_link_only` — cite and link, do not copy the source body;
- `manual_review_notes_only` — CG staff create original notes from lawfully accessed material;
- `official_reference` — current official documentation used for source-backed facts;
- `research_candidate` — useful but not yet verified or rights-cleared.

## Public-domain advertising and persuasion candidates

### 1. Scientific Advertising — Claude C. Hopkins

- Publisher/source: Library of Congress
- Canonical item: https://www.loc.gov/item/23009362/
- Date: 1923
- Rights evidence: Library of Congress states the books in the collection are public domain and free to use and reuse.
- Access: image scans/IIIF; OCR quality must be checked before ingestion.
- Candidate themes:
  - advertising as salesmanship;
  - specificity;
  - testing;
  - sampling;
  - headlines;
  - tracking response;
  - avoiding unmeasurable vanity.
- Limitations:
  - century-old examples and assumptions;
  - not a source for current platforms, privacy, attribution or social behaviour;
  - historical principles need modern ethical and legal review.

## South African legal and regulatory sources

### Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008

- Official source: https://www.gov.za/documents/consumer-protection-act
- Source type: official_reference.
- Coverage targets:
  - unfair or misleading marketing;
  - consumer information;
  - pricing and promotional claims;
  - direct marketing;
  - prohibited practices.
- Agent rule:
  - legal claims require current legal review;
  - the AI may flag risk and cite the Act but must not present itself as legal counsel.

### Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013

- Research target: official Information Regulator and government text.
- Coverage targets:
  - lawful processing;
  - consent and other lawful bases;
  - direct marketing;
  - lead data minimisation;
  - security safeguards;
  - operator responsibilities;
  - data-subject rights;
  - retention and deletion.
- Product implications:
  - every lead source should preserve consent/source evidence where available;
  - exports should be access-controlled and auditable;
  - client isolation is mandatory;
  - no lead data may become general training knowledge.

### Advertising Regulatory Board Code of Advertising Practice

- Official source: https://www.arb.org.za/
- Source type: official_reference.
- Coverage targets:
  - honest and truthful advertising;
  - substantiation;
  - misleading claims;
  - comparative advertising;
  - offence and discrimination;
  - sector appendices;
  - social/influencer disclosure where applicable.

## Candidate Master Skill Card themes

### Strategy

- objective before channel;
- market and customer research;
- segmentation, targeting and positioning;
- offer-market fit;
- awareness, consideration and conversion roles;
- differentiation and proof;
- channel selection by buyer journey;
- campaign hypothesis and success criteria.

### Copywriting

- clarity before cleverness;
- specific proof over vague superlatives;
- headline/first-line role;
- problem, desire, mechanism, proof, offer and CTA;
- objections;
- risk reversal;
- readable structure;
- claims requiring substantiation;
- adapting tone without losing meaning.

### Creative direction

- message hierarchy;
- visual attention and comprehension;
- demonstration;
- product visibility;
- branding without overwhelming the message;
- format-native adaptation;
- creative testing variables;
- production feasibility.

### Paid advertising

- campaign objective and conversion definition;
- intent and audience logic;
- landing-page alignment;
- conversion tracking health;
- primary versus secondary conversions;
- lead quality feedback;
- attribution limitations;
- budget and learning-period caution;
- creative fatigue as an observed pattern, not a universal timer;
- incrementality as a stronger causal question than platform attribution alone.

### Website and lead generation

- message match from ad to landing page;
- single clear next action;
- form friction versus qualification;
- trust signals;
- mobile usability;
- speed and technical health;
- lead response time as an operational factor;
- submitted, contacted, qualified, converted and lost as distinct stages;
- cost per lead versus cost per qualified/converted lead.

### Reporting

- source definitions before interpretation;
- unavailable is not zero;
- comparison validity;
- data latency;
- model and methodology changes;
- observation versus causation;
- actionable recommendation tied to evidence.
