## September — retrospective learning proposal

### Client-facing direction to review

September introduced the next festival's application routes and a bursary
opportunity. Our next move is to make each opportunity easier to understand and
act on: explain who it is for, what the applicant needs to prepare and where to
find the correct application. Keep each programme distinct rather than asking
people to decode one broad festival announcement.

### Strategic decision and learning

The proposed outcome is more useful, correctly routed application enquiries.
Programme participation is the business context; the precise priority and
definition of a useful enquiry still need the festival team's agreement.
Do not describe this as a September plan approved before the work happened.

The September23 bursary announcement's observed Facebook direct interactions
were higher than the September4 general applications announcement's within this
two-post comparison. This supports testing a more opportunity-specific treatment;
it does not prove bursaries outperform every programme, why people responded,
incremental reach, applications or a causal effect. Different publication dates,
audiences/distribution and unknown exposure prevent a controlled comparison.
The late reminder is a separate historical observation, not part of the
published September1–23 results or proof that reminders cannot work.

### What to keep / change / learn

- Keep a real opportunity and clear next step at the centre of the creative.
- Change broad multi-programme announcements into distinct route explanations,
  using the actual verified requirement rather than a generic inspiration quote.
- Ask the programme team which application-route questions recur. Until they
  answer, confusion about routes is a hypothesis, not a proven audience finding.
- Keep community support relevant, but do not let congratulatory posts replace
  the practical application information people need.
- Do not promise better applications from social reactions. Assess route-specific
  enquiries or application outcomes only if the team can supply exact evidence.
