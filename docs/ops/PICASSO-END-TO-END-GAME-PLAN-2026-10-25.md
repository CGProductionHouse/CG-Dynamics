## Intended staff experience

An authorized staff member tells **company ChatGPT Work**: “PICASSO, take the approved shoot in this OneDrive folder, prepare a private review, and show me the cull and edits.” PICASSO resolves the exact folder and job ID, confirms the files are available on the MSI, runs local analysis and editing, and presents a contact sheet plus exceptions. After the photography quality gate, the staff member authorizes an internal Pixieset review collection. PICASSO checks its privacy settings and posts the review link to the existing **Picasso approvals** WhatsApp group. Reviewers identify specific filenames and changes; PICASSO creates a new revision, QC checks it, republishes only approved replacements, and posts a revision note. Final client release remains a separate staff decision.

The approval-group post is an internal review handoff. It must never be treated as authorization to send a client gallery, change client-facing access, or delete originals.

## Narrow workflow to implement and prove

1. **Intake.** Staff chooses one approved OneDrive shoot folder and confirms the owner/client and intended internal reviewers. PICASSO records its canonical OneDrive item identity, account, local path, source file count, total size, estimated working space, and immutable source hashes. Pin only that folder when the Windows sync identity matches; otherwise fetch only the exact selected files through the delegated connector into a private local source folder and compare every local size/SHA-256 to OneDrive metadata. Keep source provenance private. Do not recurse through all `Clients` or hydrate multi-terabyte libraries.
2. **Private job.** Create `job-id/input-reference`, `manifest.json`, `previews/`, `revisions/r01/`, and `exports/` outside the synced source folder. Originals stay read-only. Record tool versions, colour profile, orientation, hashes, cull decisions, settings, errors, and review author. Use a per-job size quota and leave room for originals, Lightroom previews/catalog, revision masters, and final JPEGs. No automatic deletion.
3. **Cull and finish.** Use Lightroom Classic Assisted Culling with non-destructive Pick/Unflagged/Review decisions and inspect burst stacks manually for documentary value. Run scene groups separately (backlit, daylight, mixed warm, low light, already good). Apply approved presets/AI masks conservatively, inspect face/skin and clipped channels, and export sRGB high-quality JPEG once per approved revision. Use Photoshop only on duplicate masters for specific temporary blemishes. If Lightroom automation via Computer Use proves too fragile, keep the runner as an advisory first pass and let staff finish in Lightroom. The Lightroom SDK is mainly an extension point for export, publish, metadata and UI, not evidence of a supported unattended Develop/AI-culling API. [Adobe Lightroom Classic SDK](https://developer.adobe.com/lightroom-classic).
4. **Local QC gate.** Show contact sheet, before/after pairs, REVIEW/ALTERNATE reasons, failure count, dimensions, colour space and EXIF status. Staff approves which files may leave the local job. The 25-photo varied-lighting test precedes 100 and 1,000. Do not upload all culls or private working files.
5. **Internal Pixieset review.** Staff authorizes a new, clearly named **internal review** collection. Lightroom plugin or normal owner browser uploads the approved exports. Confirm image count and filenames, collection status, hidden homepage, password, download settings, watermark if desired, and storage. The plugin's Preview result is then deliberately published for the named internal reviewers. Verify a signed-out visitor can view only the intended collection with its password, then capture its direct link. Keep final client gallery separate until final signoff.
6. **Approval-group post.** In the already signed-in WhatsApp Web account, verify the group title and current membership with staff. Prepare a compact message containing job ID, revision, collection link, count, deadline if supplied, and an instruction to cite filenames or use Pixieset Favorite Notes. Confirm exact recipient and link privacy at send time. Post one message, then verify it appears in the intended group. Do not use browser DOM scraping, reverse-engineered endpoints, or an unofficial WhatsApp automation package.
7. **Amendments.** Turn feedback into a change list keyed by image filename/manifest ID, reviewer, requested edit, and decision. Ask for clarification when feedback is vague or conflicts with identity/skin constraints. Make revisions from original or Lightroom edit state, never from an already JPEG-encoded export. Save new revision outputs and a diff/contact sheet. Staff QC approves each changed image; use Pixieset republish or controlled replacement, verify no unexpected removals/duplicates, and post a revision summary to the same group. Record explicit final internal approval before separate client delivery.
