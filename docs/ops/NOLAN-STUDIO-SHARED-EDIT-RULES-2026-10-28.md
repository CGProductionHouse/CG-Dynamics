# Nolan Studio shared editing rules — PC and MSI

Owner correction: 28 October 2026. Read with the canonical editorial skill and
PR #875's current technique-status board. These explicit owner rules supersede
older optional/default wording; job ownership and release permissions stay scoped.

## Required editorial baseline

1. **Use the client's approved OneDrive logo animation for the outro.** Inspect
   the actual animation first. A new static-logo card is not a substitute.
   Owner-authorised removal of long letter close-ups must preserve the actual
   reveal, readable settled mark and identity. Mute its baked audio unless
   specifically approved; carry the chosen music through the ending.
2. **Build picture on the music's beats.** Audition/select the cue before locking
   picture. Save frame-accurate beat/downbeat markers. Put cuts and panel reveals
   on those markers; longer holds span deliberate beat multiples. Check exported
   rhythm and ending. A BPM guess or waveform is not listening. A real conflict
   with intelligible dialogue needs an explicit recorded exception, not a silent
   disregard of either requirement.
3. **Use a full-height three-shot stack for horizontal footage.** Top, middle and
   bottom contain three different relevant moving videos. Reveal one, two, three
   on the music; the completed layout fills the portrait frame. Keep each action
   readable and moving. Do not use a lone horizontal strip with empty padding,
   duplicate one source three times, or imply unrelated shoots are one event.
   Preserve separate source, in/out, orientation and crop records for each panel.
4. **Choose music for the setting and buyer.** Audition beginning, middle and
   intended ending. Avoid goofy, awkward or novelty cues under grounded product
   stories. A South African setting does not automatically call for stereotypical
   or overtly African music. Metadata is not proof of musical fit or rights.
5. **Match exposure and colour on the actual selects.** Check product colour,
   faces, highlights, shadows and white balance at full and phone size. Apply a
   coherent cinematic finish through source-specific corrections, not a blanket
   dark filter. Replace poorly exposed/clipped footage when better material
   exists. Lowering white levels cannot restore clipped texture. Preserve brand
   identity instead of grading the logo into another colour.
6. **Screen the full relevant source folder before locking selects.** Inventory
   the files and actual readable evidence. Look beyond the first convenient
   clips for meaningful action and clean in/out points. Reject camera resets,
   awkward performances, duplicate gestures and poor exposure. Contact sheets
   are triage, not complete normal-speed review. Verify upright orientation per
   file before stabilisation; a folder-wide rotation assumption is unsafe.
7. **Preserve owner-liked work.** Record a keep/change list from exact feedback.
   Keep a successful text treatment when music, layout or colour needs repair.
   Do not infer whole-video rejection when only a cover was reviewed or the
   review document could not be opened.

## Cover and review delivery

- Fill the phone's 9:16 cover with the actual image. Compose the product, short
  title and intact logo inside centered profile/feed crops; inspect 3:4, 4:5 and
  square fallbacks at small grid size as well as full-screen. A narrow landscape
  band with blurred top/bottom padding fails this owner's cover requirement even
  when the numeric dimensions are correct. Verify the actual posting preview
  before publication. Preserve an owner-selected approved Canva export exactly.
- Include a **directly playable HD VIDEO media message** in the exact verified
  **CG Nolan Approvals** group. A document alone is not usable phone review.
  Retain the high-quality document/master where required. Put only the complete
  proposed post caption on forwardable video media; internal notes stay separate.
  Verify play control, duration and HD state on the sent message. When uncertain,
  download that actual media through ordinary UI and probe it; an HD filename is
  not proof. Do not claim a quality selector was used when none was visible.
- Send the cover separately as an **HD IMAGE**, with no message caption. Send a
  brief production note separately, and verify target/type/Sent or Delivered.
  Neither silence nor delivery means client/public approval.
- This workstation is **Nolan Studio PC** or **Nolan PC**. The owner corrected
  the mistaken BC signature. MSI remains a separate workstation/lane.
- Complete already-authorised handoff instead of stopping at a local render or
  repeatedly asking for permission already given. Never invent approval, human
  listening, native application editability or missing production codes.

## One shared library, honest evidence

GitHub is the versioned common instruction source; Drive is a mirror. Both PCs
fetch the same canonical skill/technique branch until merge, read current status
and negative controls, and hash-check installation using the existing sync helper.
A downloaded card is not mastery. Promote a new method only with a bounded proof
and stated failure boundary. Link these owner rules from both handovers and the
owning PR; keep actual client feedback/source/audio/send receipts private.

This does not authorise changes to the other PC's active jobs or branch. Native
staff handover still requires actual application save/reopen/export proof. No
client media/credentials in Git, unapproved external client-media AI upload,
purchase, API spend, cloud build, storage change or unattended monitor is granted.
