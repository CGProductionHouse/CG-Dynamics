exact, current, client-safe view of its confirmed package; a substantive
source-backed current/next-month strategy ready for human review (or an honest
unavailable/once-off state); real posting dates disclosed through Client

performance and connected-service presentation; and a tested login on the
actual changed build. No published content, date, metric, asset, entitlement,
or recipient may be inferred from a draft, private folder, provider connection,
old report, or local fixture. Client invitations follow individual acceptance.
