## Adobe: included credits are NOT Higgsfield credits

[Adobe's current generative credits FAQ, 30 Sep 2026](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/apps/generative-ai/generative-credits-faq.html):
- Creative Cloud **Pro** individual: **4,000 monthly premium-generation credits** and unlimited standard generations.
- Creative Cloud **Standard**: **25 monthly credits**.
- Premiere single-app: **500/month** for some older subscription vintages (before 17 June 2025), **25/month** for newer ones. **Do not infer plan from Adobe app install or the legacy account age.**
- Different **subscriptions under the SAME Adobe account**: Adobe says generative credit allocations **stack**; Adobe accounts held under different IDs are **not** represented as a single automatically pooled balance. Teams/enterprise accounts generally are not pooled across users by default.
- Available allowance is account-specific and must be read from **Adobe Account → profile/avatar → generative credit remaining**. Adobe also shows usage in Photoshop's Generative Credits Usage panel. Do not infer the user's actual balance here. Monthly included credits don't roll over.
- Adobe may offer **two complimentary video generations** for some plans without premium access; Adobe Firefly free users have limited daily free-model access. Access/number is variable, and claiming complimentary != approval to use it.
- Adobe says it will not purchase additional generative credits automatically; however, Nolan still must NOT spend existing allotted credits without explicit owner approval.
- **Current published Firefly model video rates** (examples, must reverify at selection time): Generate Video 1080p 24fps **100 credits/s**, 720p **50 credits/s**, 540p **20 credits/s**. So a 5s 1080p clip may cost **500 Adobe credits** and a 5s 540p clip **100 credits** at those rates. Generative Extend in Premiere: 4K30 **175/s**, 4K24 **150/s**, 1080p30 **125/s**, 1080p24 **100/s**, 720p30 **75/s**, 720p24 **50/s**. Partner models may vary. These are *allowance consumption*, not automatic cash charges.
- [Adobe Generative Extend FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/africa/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-with-generative-ai/generative-extend-faq.html): generates extra frames by sending a 1.5–3s clip segment to the cloud, leaves original intact, and does not extend spoken dialogue. The clip still needs rights/privacy approval. Works differently from creating a brand-new video from text.
- **Adobe allowances cannot be exchanged for Higgsfield usage**; they are separate companies and account ledgers.
