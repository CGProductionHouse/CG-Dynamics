## What Nolan must be able to notice

A trend needn't be a trending song. Useful signals:
- **Visual/motion format:** an emerging action format, transition, phrase structure, comedic POV, transition visual, animation mechanic or editing rhythm that can be recreated with CG-owned footage/assets **without taking a creator's actual clip**.
- **Client buying situation:** insurance risk awareness, logistics/delivery anticipation, a local event, school-year or travel season, car/food/property practical moment; find an angle where client product/service genuinely belongs.
- **Seasonal event or public holiday:** verify exact South African school/public holiday dates, audience relevance, promotion timeline and any cultural sensitivity. Only invent a holiday concept if current client actually cares.
- **News event:** distinguish confirmed facts from unverified rumours. Use mainstream South African reporting + relevant official authority and date. No using someone else's death/disaster/tragedy or polarising political claim for opportunistic clicks.
- **Competitive pattern:** Facebook/Instagram/TikTok posts and public Meta Ad Library indicate *what has been posted/advertised*, not conversion rates or comprehensive account performance. Analyse visual territory to avoid repetition and find a distinctive original angle.
- **Owner-origin microtrend:** Germoparts delivery example from owner, with a fast humorous trend reinterpretation. Specific trend name/audio and published performance are NOT supplied or verified here. Do not invent a live trend claim from that example.

## Triage quickly, don't send a list of every meme to the owner

Each signal gets a **trend candidate card** only when the agent can answer:

```text
Observed: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM SAST + URL(s) + original publication/event dates
Mechanic: what viewers recognise and WHY it works without copying source footage
Momentum: firsthand examples / format/industry region recency, quality of evidence
Client fit: exact client identity, currently offered service and buyer question
Strategy fit: current month, package, previous posts, current approved constraints
Idea: one original headline/hook, 3-beat video structure, real proof, logo/CTA
Execution: footage/archive availability, stock/brand kit/animation fallback, sound rights
Risk: politics, defamation, accident/tragedy, insurance/financial claims, competitors,
      minors/sensitive persons, misleading endorsement, privacy/permission and backlash
Confidence: high | medium | low; what remains unknown
Urgency: use today / next 48h / evergreen / expired (planning judgement, not SLA)
Approvals: named client claims/offer, strategy conflict, rights, human publish decision
Decision: DRAFT_PROPOSAL | RESEARCH_MORE | REJECT | EXPIRED
```

**Hard quality filters:** not relevant to this client; trend already stale; unverifiable facts; inauthentic brand fit; no legal music; sensitive/political/opportunistic use; generic copying; fake customer/job/result; another client already has a near-identical post; brand tone conflicts. If any blocker cannot be resolved rapidly, reject or use a different hook. A brand need not hop on every trend.

- **Concept:** prepare a thumbnail/hook, one original script/beat map, source-use declaration and risk check; **same-day idea** when actually urgent, no implied guaranteed publish.
- **Create:** use existing #862 Premiere MCP and #859 craft, verified native brand MOGRT, captions/sound and archive/stock/Canva illustrations. Give owner a short decision point if strategy or rights need approval; no automatic post or external claim. An owner-approved fast concept can go to production immediately.
- **Retrospective:** see real client performance and owner feedback later when connected metrics exist, record idea/trend age and how quickly it was executed, promote transferable *visual mechanism* to editorial skill only after reviewed result. Do not claim trending popularity caused reach.

## Important platform/music caveat

[TikTok official commercial music guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library) says its CML tracks are free for in-platform business posts; this **does not grant blanket cross-platform/syndicated rights**. Before CG exports one master for Instagram/Facebook/TikTok, use separately-cleared universal audio, or prepare distinct platform-audio mixes/licences. Recreating a catchy trend with one's **own visual premise and approved audio** is safer than copying original copyrighted content. The SA trend radar is an inspiration/strategy tool, not an infringement engine.
