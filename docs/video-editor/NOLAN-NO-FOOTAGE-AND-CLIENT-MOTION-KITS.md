## Ambition

Nolan must create excellent **brand-specific video even when a scheduled shoot/content run was missed**, while staying faithful to package scope and the owner's actual brand/strategy direction. Creative ambition is not a licence to invent footage, past client work, claims, shoot dates, or strategy approval.

CG's five real video modes:

| Mode | Editorial first principle | Likely craft palette | Special QA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cinematic / B-roll film** | Show place, product, people and atmospheric progression through *best authentic visual moments* | Source screening, camera motion relationships, honest screen direction, J-cuts, selective slow-motion, graded holds and coherent soundscapes | Avoid empty mood, repetitive slow-motion, false location continuity |
| **Promotional/commercial** | Buyer need → proposition → evidence → brand recall → clear action | Strong opener, legible proof, selective editable hero type, purposeful pace and CTA | No fabricated price/offer/service promise, approved claims and contact details |
| **Talking-head / interview / testimonial** | Preserve the speaker's genuine meaning, clarity and character | Transcript review + frame/audio review, safe silence cleanup, J/L cuts, verified captions, restrained B-roll and natural CapCut-style skin touch-up only if appropriate | No deceptively reordered speech, chopped syllables or unnatural facial retouch |
| **Fast social trend / reactive** | Recognisable current mechanic adapted to a *relevant* client truth rapidly enough to matter | Distinct hook, authentic comedic/action performance, concise text, matching beat/cut, platform-aware original/cleared sound | Verify trend is still alive, suitability, safety, commercial music/rights, not copy a creator verbatim |
| **Motion-led / stock-led / no-shoot video** | **Story through brand and graphic motion, not footage quantity**; let a strong proposition drive the reveal | Dynamic type + kinetic icon flows, radial/wheel/diagram animation, logo/shape morphs, layers of product photos/stock/archived shots, charts only if data true, dramatic visual transitions, separate SFX/music | Contemporary original design; no 2019 slideshow presets, false illustration-as-client-work, fake case studies, stale archive reuse |

**These modes may mix.** A promotional film can include 8s of animated insurance coverage flow; a cinematic hotel video can have one bold type-only brand sequence; a trend may rely on an existing image. Build a creative mechanism for the actual client first, not a rigid genre effect chain.

## Motion-first brief when footage is limited or absent

A meaningful motion-led video is **not** random moving typography. Example for a **broker** (illustrative concept, not approved TBS/PSG claims):

- **Audience question:** Why would someone speak to a broker instead of guessing at cover? Don't assert a specific regulated benefit or saving until approved.
- **0–3s: hook.** One confident on-brand headline + clean object/icon rotation/insurance category morph, establishing tension/curiosity.
- **3–10s: readable animated explanation.** Three *actually offered* service categories may appear through a radial/wheel/diagram system with brand colors; layered human/vehicle/home stock is clearly illustrative, never framed as an actual claim paid by the client.
- **10–19s: proof/positioning.** Replace generic words with approved credentials/service process/client-backed differentiators. Use verified real archived team/office photos if available; brand motion bridges missing footage.
- **19–26s: relief/benefit.** A simpler visual state/voice line; not unsubstantiated protection or money-saved promises.
- **26–30s: logo payoff + exact approved CTA.** Branded animated logo, website/contact, approved legal footnote if required; music resolution, no arbitrary end.
- **Creative alternative:** purely graphical abstract client-identity animation when there is absolutely no legitimate real media; keep source/brand honest.

Timing is an **illustrative planning example**, not a fixed recipe. Decide based on audience comprehension, footage, platform, requested duration, claim approval. Nolan should be able to make the explainer *visually exciting* without inventing visuals with AI credits.

## Client brand packaging: reusable on the next video

**Logical per-client motion kit** (metadata/proposal only; do NOT create folders/upload/publish now):
- **Identity:** exact client ID, client short code from configured `clients.short_code`, version/date, approved point of contact, safe source provenance and permissions.
- **Look:** approved HEX/RGB brand colours, gradients/secondary neutrals, **licensed** font PostScript name/fallbacks, logo variants dark/light, protected safe area, visual texture, icon/shapes/supergraphics, composition and colour do/don't examples.
- **Motion:** named **Premiere/After Effects MOGRT** logo reveals, lower-third, headline reveal, kinetic three-point list, number/wheel/diagram transition, icon burst, CTA/website/social cards, end slate. Expose actual text/font/size/color/media-replacement controls. Keep usereditable master templates, project/source files, version and used-font dependencies. Cross-client style reuse is allowed for CG-owned generic technique, *not* leakage of actual branding.
- **Sound:** properly licensed reusable client sonic mark, music mood notes, whoosh/foley mapping with per-platform rights.
- **Archive:** approved first-party photos, posters, past exports, footage location/shot ledgers and last-use log. Track previously-posted clip IDs and repetition tolerance; prefer unused moments and meaningful new pairings, not stale recycling.
- **Canva roundtrip:** original client-owned logo/SVG or rights-cleared transparent PNG **as assets**; export still diagrams/backgrounds where allowed; rebuild editable typographic motion natively in Premiere/MOGRT (Canva MP4/GIF is a rendered/baked asset, NOT editable text inside Premiere). Record original Canva design link and asset permission, not an unaudited pile of files.
- **Distribution:** the same asset identity should be findable from Dynamics Brand Identity/OneDrive; Premiere graphics through Adobe Libraries or client-safe local folders. No duplicate DB/file authority. Reserve portability and dependency manifest for eventual desktop move; **do not build packaging/installers now**.

**Canva licence trap:** [Canva content terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/) generally permit exported full *designs* in commercial videos but **Pro library components cannot automatically be extracted/distributed as standalone asset packs** or trademarked. Export the client's *owned/uploaded* intellectual property, CG-made elements or separately-licensed assets, not standalone proprietary Canva Pro illustrations. [Canva Brand Kits](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-kit/) centrally store logo/font/colour; [export file types](https://www.canva.com/help/download-file-types/) support SVG/PNG, but SVG/transparency controls may require an existing paid Canva plan. Don't assume every poster can be disassembled legally. A Canva MP4 animation is a baked segment, not editable Premiere copy.

**Premiere native path:** [Adobe AE authoring](https://helpx.adobe.com/africa/after-effects/desktop/motion-graphics/work-with-motion-graphics-templates/creating-motion-graphics-templates.html) can expose editable font family/size/colour/data/media in MOGRT. [Premiere Media Replacement](https://helpx.adobe.com/ca/premiere/desktop/add-text-images/use-motion-graphics-templates/media-replacement-in-motion-graphics-templates.html) swaps photo/logo/video within predesigned animation; [Premiere libraries](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-text-images/use-motion-graphics-templates/organize-motion-graphics-templates.html) organise packages. Installed #862 26.3.2 verified SOME MOGRT text/font/size keyframes via CEP. **MOGRT authoring or media replacement through MCP for any new pack must be individually tested; Adobe UI documentation isn't Codex API proof.**

## CG video quality & approvals

- One **real brand**, one visual argument, one strong promise, one measurable next action. Conspicuous on-brand cues early enough to be attributed, not generic motion stock.
- Every visual labelled **original archived / current camera / Canva-owned / stock illustrative / synthetic** privately; no fabricated physical installation, customer, claim, chart, insurance payout, price or performance.
- Kinetic typography remains legible at phone size; edit to meaning and voice/music accents, not motion noise. Rotating/wheel icons must represent approved categories. Clean hierarchy and rhythm (burst→hold→resolve).
- Client logos/colour not inferred from web scrapings when internal approved assets exist; do not invent brand approvals.
- A proposal is not a confirmed strategy, contracted deliverable or permission to publish. Review/approve before client send.
- Save reusable *generic craft* lesson in #859 when approved; keep actual client feedback in permitted private client context, not public GitHub.
