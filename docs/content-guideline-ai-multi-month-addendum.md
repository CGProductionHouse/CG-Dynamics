# Content Guideline AI and Multi-Month Shoot Addendum

Last updated: 30 July 2026

This addendum records a confirmed correction from CA and must be read with `AGENTS.md`, `docs/current-product-game-plan.md`, and `docs/cg-dynamics-page-vision-and-milestones.md` before changing Content Workflow or Content Guidelines.

## Core correction

A Content Run is a real shoot session. It is not the same thing as one calendar month and must never be limited to only that month's deliverables.

CG Production House may travel for a shoot and capture several months of content in advance. One Content Run may therefore contain any number of ordered videos covering several future months.

## Required data relationships

- Keep exactly one canonical Content Guideline per Content Run.
- Allow the guideline to contain an unrestricted practical number of ordered videos.
- Each video may link independently to:
  - one target publication month;
  - one specific Client Schedule deliverable where available;
  - a campaign, season, event or evergreen purpose;
  - its own planned publication date later when known.
- Videos inside one guideline may target different months.
- Do not derive video quantity from the shoot month or package quantity for one month.
- Do not force every planned video to already have a Client Schedule record before it can exist in the guideline.
- Preserve one-to-one linking to a Client Schedule item where a canonical deliverable exists, but allow a planned/unallocated state until scheduling is confirmed.
- Never duplicate a video merely because it moves to another month.

## Content Guideline structure

The guideline is one complete working document for the shoot, containing multiple ordered videos. Each video should support:

- video number and name;
- target month or months;
- linked Client Schedule deliverable where available;
- objective;
- hook;
- complete script or dialogue;
- scene-by-scene shot instructions;
- on-screen text;
- props, products and people required;
- filming location;
- visual references;
- call to action;
- owner/editor;
- internal notes;
- approval and production state.

Never invent missing scripts or claim that incomplete AI output is approved content.

## AI must actively assist planning

The Content Guideline must not behave as a passive blank form. It should provide contextual, research-driven assistance for the selected client and planned coverage period.

AI suggestions should consider:

- the selected client and industry;
- the months the team intends to cover;
- South African and local seasonal context;
- relevant public holidays, school periods, pay cycles, industry moments and known events;
- client campaigns, products, services and current priorities;
- the client's package and available future deliverables;
- previous approved concepts, hooks, scripts and visual treatments;
- recent content performance where connected;
- repetition risk across the same client and other active clients;
- evergreen opportunities that can be filmed in advance.

The system should suggest useful content opportunities, not generate generic filler.

## Required AI workflow

1. Staff select the client and Content Run.
2. Staff choose the future coverage window, for example August through January.
3. The system loads canonical client, package, schedule, historical guideline and approved Marketing Library context.
4. The AI produces grouped suggestions by month, campaign or evergreen purpose.
5. Each suggestion shows its reasoning and likely target month.
6. Staff can accept, reject, edit, reorder or combine suggestions.
7. Accepted suggestions become draft videos inside the one canonical guideline.
8. AI may help draft hooks, scripts and shot plans, but all output remains draft until reviewed.
9. Staff can add unlimited manual videos alongside AI suggestions.
10. Publication or approval remains a deliberate document-level action.

## Calendar and scheduling boundaries

- The Content Run remains linked to the actual shoot date in CG Calendar.
- The shoot date does not define the publication month of every video.
- Each video carries its own target month or linked Client Schedule item.
- CG Calendar remains the operational shoot/event calendar.
- Client Schedule remains the canonical monthly deliverable and publishing plan.
- Do not merge CG Calendar and Client Schedule.

## User experience requirements

The Content area should make it obvious that staff are planning one shoot across a chosen coverage window.

At minimum, provide:

- coverage start and end month controls;
- Add video without an artificial monthly cap;
- AI research suggestions grouped by target month/campaign;
- clear target-month labels on every video;
- direct linking to matching Client Schedule deliverables;
- a visible unallocated/planned state when no schedule item exists yet;
- duplicate/repetition warnings;
- ordered drag or move controls;
- one complete guideline view for the full shoot.

Do not present a single month selector as though it limits the entire Content Run.

## Acceptance examples

- A Cape Town shoot in July can contain videos targeting August, September, October, November, December and January.
- The guideline can hold several videos for one month and none for another.
- Staff can add more videos than the current monthly package quantity when the shoot is deliberately producing future content.
- AI suggestions change when the selected coverage months change.
- Historical concepts are checked before suggesting near-duplicates.
- Every accepted AI suggestion remains editable and auditable.
- Missing scripts remain visibly incomplete and are never fabricated as final.

## Scope warning

Navigation consolidation must not falsely declare Content complete while the guideline remains a static month-bound form. Any current mission touching Content must preserve this model and either implement it or clearly report it as outstanding launch work.
