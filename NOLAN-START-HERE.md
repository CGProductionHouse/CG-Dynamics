## The editing floor applies throughout the film

Load the exact client kit, latest approval/exclusions and live brief before a
timeline. Research the buyer, service, Bloemfontein/South African season and why
this message matters now. Check actual source-month and later public posts;
older shoots may supply a genuinely different moment/new job, never a lifted
published beat as-is. Do not ask for new filming merely because a shoot was posted.

Choose the actual first-picture/sound hook and its payoff, development and ending.
Use roughly30seconds unless the owner specifies otherwise. Select good actions
already moving and flattering completed moments; exclude dead lead-ins, pointless
details, repeated original recordings and awkward performances. Design purposeful
visual/type/UI/transition craft across the whole film from the relevant tested
cards, not a list of effects or a generic title over ordinary clips.

Respect exact brand artwork/copy restrictions (for example **TBS Brokers**, never
standalone TBS). Compact measured line spacing, deliberate mixed type, soft shadows
where appropriate, full motion-path readability. Upright before stabilisation;
steady/focused shots must be stable, intentional camera motion may remain. Match
exposure/white balance/contrast before a coherent look; no stretched faces, forced
rotated landscape, static poster filler or arbitrary stock/fire/sound effects.

Audition licensed music and check the shared use/rejection ledger. Cut to actual
musical/action cues, mute B-roll crew direction, preserve purposeful natural sound,
and prelap/fade effects across their motivated picture. Carry the chosen music
over the supplied picture-only official logo; do not decorate its animation.
Closing copy belongs before it. Check the actual final five seconds audibly.
