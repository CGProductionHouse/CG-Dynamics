## Formal look and fabric review

For a staff-reviewed daylight formal shoot, `run` accepts `--look cg-formal-warm`. Runner 0.3.11 applies moderate contrast, warmth and colour while restraining low-light frames. It records the named look in the manifest; `amend` inherits it. Use a new job folder when changing looks or source selections, and inspect the contact sheet against approved staff edits. This is a review candidate, not a universal preset or human quality approval.

Inspect obvious temporary sweat stains at full resolution. Simple local brightness and inpainting masks produced visible artifacts on a formal test, so those trials were rejected. Use supervised Photoshop work on a separate review copy for the affected clothing and record the exact result; never label a tone-only export as retouched. Preserve fabric folds, skin and permanent features.
