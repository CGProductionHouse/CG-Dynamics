# Archival bout source-selection screen — 29 October 2026

This is a bounded, **rejected picture/source screen** for the existing [fight-impact-edit family](skills/fight-impact-edit/SKILL.md) under [draft PR #910](https://github.com/CGProductionHouse/CG-Dynamics/pull/910), #874/#903. It tests whether a rights-cleared continuous historical bout can support an honest 30-second source-led cut. It is **not** a Fight Night client amendment, contemporary event claim, verified landed-blow highlight, sound design, or creative pass. No visible desktop application, client footage, upload, purchase or paid/API credit was used.

## Source rights and full-footage gate

[Wikimedia Commons archival boxing match](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boxing_Match_Gunnar_B%C3%A4rlund_vs_Denis_Juliani_at_Helsinki_3.6.1947.webm) identifies Gunnar Bärlund vs Denis Juliani, Helsinki, 3 June 1947, and credits **Yle Archives – Elävä arkisto**. It is published under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), requiring attribution, license link and indication of changes for reuse. The privately downloaded original hashes `c786b4a98523063178943483afe230bff795e99fe75d4adf78e4bb336a34ac1e`, 9,269,512 bytes, 66.286 s, VP8 960×528/25fps plus Vorbis stereo48kHz. The item page describes Finnish commentary; that is not isolated ring/camera sound. No outcome or precise contact moment was inferred from its title.

The full source was sampled at two-second intervals, plus additional 0/5/10/15/20/25/30/34/40/45/49-second pictures and actual cut frames. It is a real two-fighter bout, but the archival picture is dark, wide and interspersed with an interview/narrator insert near25–30 s. The sampled views show exchanges and corner pressure, but **do not establish a distinct sharp glove-to-target contact and response suitable for a claimed impact beat**. The source is historical newsreel, not an authorised CG event. A separately checked [CC BY-SA 3.0 “Boxer” file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boxer.webm) has an audio stream but depicts outdoor shadowboxing with no opponent; it was rejected before any pressure-pocket render. A file's audio stream alone does not establish useful original impact sound.

## Decision and remaining gates

Neither cut qualifies as a 25–35 s impact trailer: no clear timecoded landed hit with setup/contact/reaction was accepted; the archival film's narration is not original location impact sound; the jumps may misrepresent the original sequence; no independent normal-speed/phone/heard judgment exists. **Reject R01/R02 as client-style source edits and retain plain as factual reference only.** This screen advances source triage and ethical cut accounting in the existing family, not mastery. A valid future event requires actual authorized event masters, verified distinct contact moments and outcomes, original camera/ring audio, appropriate rights, a clean straight-cut control, heard matched-level sound design, full-speed/physical-phone review and staff-editable native handover. MSI's Fight amendments remain untouched.
