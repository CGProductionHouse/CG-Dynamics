**25 October 2026. Silent stock technique test, no event impact claim.** The [motocross reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@josiahlebante14/video/7635639920148761876) uses objects/dirt passing close to lens to carry motion through cuts. This tests whether a real glove sweep can do that job without a plugin blur, synthetic roll or cutout. The two source shots have different performers and are **not** one real exchange.

The sampled frames show the early cut jumps from a readable guard to a large foreground glove. The alternate waits until #40970's own glove is already sweeping across frame, then joins #40973's glove near the lens. It is a more motivated visual handoff on these samples. No optical flow, artificial blur, mask or independently floating edge was added; the foreground is native to the source. The output is silent because these licensed clips have no camera audio. This does **not** prove a complete reference-quality transition: the glove does not cover the whole frame, and the two unrelated staged bouts could be read as one continuous punch. **Do not use this exact A/B in a client trailer.** A final event version needs two source moments whose bout/time/order are clear, or a visual break that makes the change of moment honest, plus normal-speed phone and sound review.
