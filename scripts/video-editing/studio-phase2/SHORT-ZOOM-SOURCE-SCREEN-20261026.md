## 26 October: portrait carpenter punch-in A/B — rejected for editorial use

Four local 720 × 1280, 24000/1001 fps [Mixkit Free License](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/) carpenter sources were contact-sheeted at five moments each. The private `carpenter-contact.png` shows the actual frames. Two 4.000 s, 24 fps, silent pairs were then rendered to test **only** a hard 8–14% punch-in against a three-frame ramp at the same source time and focal point. Both versions in each pair decoded fully. No new plugin, cross-shot continuity, motion blur, audio review or native editor handover was involved.
