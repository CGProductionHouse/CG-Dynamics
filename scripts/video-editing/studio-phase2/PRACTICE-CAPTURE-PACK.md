# Capture pack for visual skills that need real camera inputs

Use a disposable non-client phone shoot to unlock the capture-dependent tutorial families. The current local failures are documented in the cards; another synthetic drawing or unrelated stock montage cannot prove these camera mechanics. Keep originals, actual fps/orientation, source sound and consent/rights with the private practice project. No client, purchase, cloud upload or plugin is required.

| Skill | Minimal real capture | Pass before editing | Existing failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filmed last-frame screen reveal | Shot A ends on a stable readable frame. Play its actual tail on a physical phone/monitor; begin shot B with that display covering the lens, then pull away to reveal a related second place in one continuous take. | Screen truly fills camera frame, exposure/orientation match, device/hand leave through an edge; save the displayed tail asset. | Current green-screen stock phone never covers the lens. A digital floating rectangle is rejected. |
| Grounded freeze | Lock the camera. Record an empty background plate, then the subject enters/stops on the same floor with full limbs and contact shadow visible. Keep background motion if the effect needs time continuing around the subject. | No camera shift between plate and subject; clean limb/shadow edges and sufficient negative space for the word. | No clean plate leaves duplicate heads or a spotlight patch inside the matte. |
| Repeated-object match cut | Film the same cup/book/phone at three related places, using a framing grid and the same hand direction/gesture. Record a few seconds before/after each action. | Object shape/scale/position agree at the cut; the new locations serve one story. | Two unrelated key types are a semantic match only. |
| Physical stop motion | Fixed phone/tripod and fixed exposure/focus. Move one owned object along a surface with a small consistent increment, capturing stills with hands absent. Keep contact shadow. | No camera bump, lighting flicker or floating cut edge; retain all original stills in order. | Current continuous-versus-frame-hold drawing is a temporal surrogate, not physical stop motion. |
| Cinematic journey continuity | Same person/wardrobe, one route and destination. Capture an orienting wide, a detail/action, a linked change of angle and an arrival/payoff. Preserve movement direction. | Same identity and geography, no reset of gesture at cuts, subject survives portrait framing. | A second stock walker changes protagonists; three crops of one angle do not create new coverage. |
| Natural depth type / object handoff | Lock or slowly move the camera while a real foreground hand/object crosses a stable title area. Capture a clean plate if possible. | Foreground edge readable at full size; enough uncrossed glyph remains to read the whole word; no face/safety action hidden. | Dark-pixel keys capture scenery; cluttered boxing mats and obscured glyphs fail before rendering. |

For every captured set, build a plain control first, then one effect variable. Save per-frame edge checks, a phone-scale view and the exact encoded preview. If a source fails its capture gate, record that failure and move to another unlocked technique. The office agent can use this pack as a practice-source assignment; it does not authorise taking over MSI client edits.
