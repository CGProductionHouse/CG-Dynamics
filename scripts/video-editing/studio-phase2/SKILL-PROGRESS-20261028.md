## Remaining work in useful order

1. New negative/band/film controls now exist. Next establish meaningful shot context and normal-speed/phone comfort, heard sound and native editable layers; do not rerun identical settings or call rejected source choices a client preset. A physical film burn and exact reference reconstruction remain unproven.
2. Source-motivated directional whip and rotational lens construction; compare with plain/zoom-only controls, protect readable subject and avoid fabricated continuity.
3. Natural foreground with clean hair/limbs/contact shadow, then meaningful type behind it. Green screen does not prove general extraction. Apply the new whole-motion boundary audit.
4. Real filmed-device last-frame/window transition. Graphic inset growth is a separate valid technique; the floating picture over a phone holder remains rejected.
5. Same-object three-take match, actual tripod stop motion and a coherent same-person journey. Existing synthetic/semantic substitutes do not meet these source gates.
6. Match separately licensed/original music, camera sound and effects to the completed visual action; independently hear the exact result. Objective level/mono/timing checks are already implemented, but cannot certify musical fit.
7. Appropriate real client application, native editable layers/stems, save/reopen/export and second-machine relink receipt for each promoted method. No office installation/application claim without an office receipt.
