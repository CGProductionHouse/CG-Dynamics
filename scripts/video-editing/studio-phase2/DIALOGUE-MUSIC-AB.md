# Voice under music: bounded duck and failed ASR acceptance gate

**25 October 2026 — six-second sound-only technical A/B.** This challenges the owner's voice/music tutorial family without pretending to finish the Fight Night soundtrack. A completely synthetic sentence, “Fight night. One moment changes everything,” was generated locally by installed Windows SAPI (`Microsoft Zira Desktop`, 22,050 Hz mono; ignored WAV SHA-256 `8a3a75bb64708afba7f8d01016574a038ee78df2110376a52062021233992814`). It is a test sentence, **not proposed event copy, narration or an AI-credit service**. Music is the previously declared, locally held [Arulo “Cat Walk” Mixkit #371](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/house/) under the [Mixkit free music licence](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/), original SHA-256 `e241cba000ce12e52cadd4c9956274e391ef1f66d1e48a5dd40bfc21645aa489`. It is **not** the rejected fight-montage track and is not proposed for the trailer. Source and exports remain ignored in `media/`.

Both mixes use the same 6 s music excerpt (source 10.425 s) and the same 3.514 s voice beginning at timeline 0.8 s. Voice gain and master gain are identical; the only change is the music envelope. Control music gain stays 0.65; candidate ramps from 0.65 to 0.13 by 0.65 s, holds through 4.45 s and returns by 4.95 s. A music-only measurement confirms that the candidate is **13.98 dB quieter** during 1.0–1.2 and 3.0–3.2 s, while 0.05–0.25 and 5.3–5.5 s match the control. Thus the automation occurred before the voice entrance and returned after it, not by abrupt on/off. Full six-second AAC exports decode; control true peak −6.1 dBTP and duck true peak −6.8 dBTP. Control M4A SHA-256 `f815ad090e257c1ad1738b949aa349f37232df01ce3f7adcf8f322eb207309fd`; duck SHA-256 `e2a29cf8f876f9aac8ae9499207bb24a66b357877ad3edcdaf3fd1b72ca7e29a`.

The initial local `faster-whisper` file-input path failed because installed PyAV rejects its `metadata_errors` argument. Passing decoded/resampled NumPy audio to the already installed engine avoided changing the system packages. Its tiny English model transcribed the synthetic voice alone and the **constant-music** mix correctly, but called the first word of both duck versions “Five” instead of “Fight.” The tested earlier duck ramp did not repair the error. This is a failed automatic *intelligibility decision*: one small ASR result is not a human listening verdict, and a measured 14 dB duck did not demonstrate improvement. Do not promote this A/B as a recommended setting or use the sentence/music for a client. No owner practice audition is requested.

Transfer rule: if the real Fight Night film contains an announcer or meaningful speech, begin with its original camera track, automate *real licensed music* around whole phrases, compare against continuous music at matched playback level, and have an independent editor hear consonants, entry and return in the completed cut. If the trailer has no important speech, this skill is not a reason to insert narration. Keep voice and music on separate staff-editable tracks. The [sound playbook](FIGHT-SOUND-PLAYBOOK.md) controls the event mix.
