## 5 November owner creative steer — the skoonma test

`Skoonma` means mother-in-law (`stiefma` means stepmother). The owner's joke is an **option**, not an instruction to force a comedy script over weak footage. Test the natural Afrikaans question **“Gaan jou kar die skoonma-toets slaag?”** against a moving car reveal. Avoid the mixed-language `skoonma-proof` unless an Afrikaans reviewer actually prefers it. The film's actual subject remains WiseRide's *general valet/detailing service*, not tyres, mechanical roadworthiness, tint or accident cover.

**Preferred opening A/B:** Start with the sharpest real WiseRide surface/detail action, then cut to a moving wide car shot. Set the question in the kit's Tomorrow Bold Italic. If the car crosses enough of the words cleanly, split the shot into background/type/foreground vehicle layers so the vehicle **passes in front of the type**. Preserve grounded wheels/body, windscreen and mirror edges; check every masked frame at full and phone size. The old trolley-through-type treatment is a construction reference, not permission to use an automatic poor cutout. Plain moving-picture/type is the fallback if the vehicle matte fails. Cue one precise door/wipe/contact transient and a brief musical pocket under the question, then bring the beat in with a real valet action. No canned sitcom voice or stock `skoonma` character.

**Payoff and possible copy, pending footage:** Turn the humorous question into a visible inside/outside valet result. One concise end line could be **“Maak jou kar reg vir Desember. Vra WiseRide oor 'n valet.”** Check the exact offer and Afrikaans with a fluent reviewer before final export. A locally recorded, approved voiceover is optional; subtitles/type must carry the story without it. The earlier `BEFORE THE LONG DRIVE`/`READY FOR THE KILOMETRES` hook table is now fallback research only. Do not assemble a repeated rinse-to-cabin sequence from the 26 October posted reel to service this new headline.

**Camera-language instruction:** Screen Franco's full source motion for a rotation, pan or wipe gesture that can lead into the next shot's matching direction. Align movement at the cut and land it on a heard beat; use speed changes only where they preserve the gesture. Stabilise shots intended to be focused/still, but do not erase deliberate travel. Compare normal-speed A/B in portrait and reject a transition that twists the car or loses the service action.

## Editorial treatment to audition, not an effect quota

- **Format:** 9:16 social film only if every selected original has a moving portrait crop that retains the hands, car, product and result. Otherwise consider a fully composed landscape film; do not force landscape strips into a vertical frame.
- **Look:** automotive contrast and controlled highlights, but correct and match exposure/white balance shot by shot first. Preserve real paint, skin, film and WiseRide brand colours. Stabilise focused or standing shots; keep deliberate moving camera energy only when it improves the action.
- **Type/graphics:** the shared WiseRide client kit identifies **Tomorrow Bold Italic** for headings and **Gruppo Regular** for the secondary line, with licensed files in its OneDrive `brand/fonts` folder. The existing supplied red/black WiseRide logo is the mark; `Automove Personal Use` may not be newly typeset commercially. Retrieve the exact logo, font files, palette values and actual Canva master `DAGsGOlBVBQ` before production. Use a strong two-level title with one word interacting with the car/hand only when there is a clean mask and credible edge. Test full resolution and phone size; reject a generic line-under-bold-font template, fake UI icons or a floating cutout. No invented palette.
- **Candidate techniques:** (1) 2–3-frame *rapid-shutter* detail sequence as an entry, with a single click/air tail on a real action; (2) *subject-silhouette prelap* at a car or worker edge to carry the next stage in, with a full-size matte check; (3) *scene-perspective type* pinned to a real car plane if tracking survives the moving crop. Each needs a plain-versus-effect A/B and must add clarity. No fire, glitch or flash for its own sake.
- **Music:** shortlist a licensed beat-forward electronic/rock or restrained automotive percussion cue with a distinct first hit, breath/pocket and useful closing phrase. The shared 5 November ledger explicitly blocks WiseRide's rejected **Funkorama** and the flat **Deep Urban** audition; **K.O.** was sourced for an earlier WiseRide private audition but never creatively accepted, so it remains a candidate only after actual listening. Avoid recycling the same candidate by default. Check `CG Production House/Video Editing System/NOLAN-MUSIC-USE-AND-REJECTIONS.csv` before lock. Catalogue mood tags alone do not choose the song. Cuts land on heard beats or intentionally skipped beats; the opening may be SFX-led if recorded in the beat sheet. The music carries through the official picture-only logo.
- **Sound:** retain only useful real car/process sound, classify and mute crew direction and silent direct-to-camera mouth movement. Layer licensed action cues by physical onset, peak and tail over adjacent clips; a wipe/peel/door sound must neither disappear abruptly at the cut nor recur without a matching action. Keep music, natural audio and effects as separable stems. Full-speed listening on the exact export is mandatory.
