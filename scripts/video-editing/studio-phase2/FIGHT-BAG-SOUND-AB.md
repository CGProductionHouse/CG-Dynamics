# Bag-contact sound: dry versus one licensed cue

**Technical timing proof only; auditory quality unreviewed.** The [dry control](proof-fight-bag-sound-dry.mp4) and [one-cue candidate](proof-fight-bag-sound-one-cue.mp4) use the **same encoded picture stream** (SHA-256 `c20cb28ed6f1f4f7b2c6f5462f18a13570eb5335b0b23b88a0ab0fb01ddeb5d2`). The stock training video has no camera-audio stream, so the dry version contains explicit silence. There is no music, synthetic bass hit, artificial crowd, or claimed Fight Night impact.

Its licence permits inclusion in a larger end product; it does not permit redistributing the source SFX on its own.
- [Reproduce](render_fight_bag_sound_ab.py): hash-check the committed practice video and ignored WAV; copy the same H.264 video stream into both MP4s. Candidate audio uses WAV source 17.15–18.40125 s, gain 0.75 and a 0.20125 s tail fade. Source RMS is nearly silent until about 17.65 s; the decoded AAC candidate first exceeds 0.01 RMS at output 0.53 s. The highest 10 ms RMS window is also at 0.53 s. No visual frame or contact timing was altered.

Both files are 720×1280, 30 video frames, 1.25125 s and fully decode. Cue file AAC is 44.1 kHz stereo; its isolated-hit measurement is −34.3 LUFS integrated and −8.3 dBTP, **not a target for a finished trailer mix**. The silence before the hit dominates integrated loudness, so boosting toward a programme LUFS number would be an error. Control SHA-256 `d1c4de0b205e35af868a764398320cc01bd0942fa4c6141398d063b744e4b827`; candidate SHA-256 `59b6f0989dd9adacd4bbc1ce22e0433a6841ed2cdd36c8a89a97142eda2085a4`.

The task's audio-output attempt again returned `audio content omitted because you do not support audio input`. **I have not heard this candidate.** Timing, waveform and peak measurements cannot establish that its bag material, room, attack, tail or spatial image fit the visible training shot. A human must hear dry and candidate at normal speed on headphones and a phone, then reject the cue if it feels fake, early, late or exaggerated. The dry control is unusually silent because the stock picture lacks production audio; the Staffy event pass should begin with its *actual camera/ring sound* and only add a licensed cue if that sound needs support. Never reuse this bag sound as evidence of a landed blow on a fighter.
