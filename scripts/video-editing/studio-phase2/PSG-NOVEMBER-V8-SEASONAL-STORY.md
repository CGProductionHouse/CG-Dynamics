## Why a Free State grower would care in November

The target viewer is a Free State summer-grain grower moving from field preparation into planting, or finishing a winter-grain harvest while planning the next crop. The practical task is real: [Grain SA's planting guidance](https://www.grainsa.co.za/take-note-of-the-climate-outlook-for-the-2024/2025-summer-season) places maize/soy planting from October to mid/late November in the eastern Free State and from mid-November through December in central/western summer-grain areas. This is a **typical regional window from a 2024 guidance article**, not a claim that rain or planting happened at a particular farm in 2026. [Grain SA's Welkom-region fact sheet](https://www.grainsa.co.za/upload/Fact-Sheet-Welkom.pdf) similarly gives an October–late December maize window; [the South African agriculture department's wheat brochure](https://nda.gov.za/phocadownloadpap/Brochures_and_Production_Guidelines/Brochure%20Wheat.pdf) places wheat harvest in November/December. Together these support a *harvest-to-planting* visual sentence, provided no stock shot is misrepresented as a PSG customer's farm.

The video should recognise that the farmer is already making time-sensitive field decisions, then offer one practical next action: call the local PSG Donald Murray team to **discuss crop insurance options for the season being planned**. It should not forecast this year's weather, imply a particular hazard is covered, promise an outcome, or pretend the staff photo records agricultural advice. The branch phone `051 813 9300` is on [the official PSG adviser profile](https://advisers.psg.co.za/financial-advice/adviser-profile/louise-pretorius). PSG group documents show crop insurance activity, but the exact local product and current approved copy remain a client sign-off gate.

The emotional path is: *the field work is already moving → one more planning conversation can be made now → a real local PSG person is reachable*. The intended response is a qualified phone enquiry, not merely an impression that farm footage looks nice. Unlike October's already-posted business-growth question, this is about the **actual November planting window** and its work. The exact words must be judged for farm fluency before client release.
