2. Watch complete originals with sound, then log exact selected in/out, useful action, rejected neighbours, prior-use result, real-client versus illustrative stock, and audio class. 3. Write a one-sentence audience → need → visible proof → action goal and test at least three **different** opening mechanisms against the first real shots. The hook must be an observable action, tension or useful question, not a client name or generic statement. Establish the visual/sound arc and ~30-second scene jobs before timeline assembly.
