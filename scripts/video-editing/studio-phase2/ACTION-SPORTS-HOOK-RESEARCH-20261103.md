## What went wrong

The proposals started with words and bolted on an effects list. They did not establish an arresting first image, a physical change worth following, a sound event, or a payoff supported by actual footage. Naming a tracking cue, split panel or speed ramp does not demonstrate a hook. The system already contained hook instructions; completing those fields was incorrectly treated as evidence of editorial judgment. More checklist fields or another renderer will not solve that mistake.

CA wants an athletic visual language comparable in ambition to football/NBA promos: purposeful layered graphics, motion, expressive people, action and sound. This does not mean copying league identities, fictional scores, basketball sounds onto turf, or disguising weak footage with effects. The old short/generic Action film remains rejected.

## How this changes the next Bloem opening

No replacement slogan is approved by this study. Build the opening around an actual clear physical moment in Franco's March/June/nested-July source pool, then decide its words. The verified five-source intake is not yet adequate to promise a complete action/payoff; continue actual moving source screening, not another montage of its weak fragments.

The working construction should identify these concrete layers before a hook proof is proposed:

1. **First picture and tension:** an expressive athlete, decisive gesture or legible contested action that works without explanatory copy. Name the exact source/in/out and why that instant creates anticipation.
2. **Visible change/payoff:** what physically changes next, and how the viewer can read it. Reveal the result from continuous/verified coverage; never manufacture a catch/score from unrelated shots.
3. **Graphic choreography:** choose a mechanism the moment earns. Possibilities supported by the reference study include a registered foreground/rear-type depth treatment, directional panel reveal, or matched detail/wide. Depth/matte needs clean source edges and no duplicate heads; graphic direction must follow actual movement. Keep essential ball/hand/contact visible. These are candidates, not effects already applied or a compulsory stack of all three.
4. **Sound choreography:** identify actual camera event, music attack/phrase, an intentional pocket and licensed transition if needed. A whoosh belongs to a visible movement; an impact belongs to real contact. Choose by listening to the real combination, not song title/BPM/peak. No song or sound is selected by this research.
5. **Exit into purpose:** the opening must lead into why this particular viewer would play at this venue and the real enquiry. Do not spend the whole30seconds repeating hook graphics or the already-posted generic multi-sport invitation.
