# Fight vertical action framing

**Lifecycle:** [stock fill-versus-fit A/B](../../FIGHT-VERTICAL-FRAMING-STUDY.md) shows a hero-shot framing failure → actual event/phone review pending → mastered **NO**.

For each candidate hit, confirm the target, striker, contact and immediate result in the original source. Test the final aspect ratio early. A frame-filling crop is useful only while those elements remain readable through the moving shot; one good still is insufficient. A full-width fit may preserve evidence but shrink the action too far for a phone.

Compare the simple fill and fit controls before trying a tracked crop, composite, zoom or graphic border. If both fail, select a closer or portrait camera angle or use the wide source for geography rather than hero impact. Never cut away the contact point and compensate with a sound effect. Keep a second reviewer and original source timecode in the edit log.

The linked proof tests one 16:9 stock shot only. It does not establish a good final layout, event contact, normal-speed readability or a client-approved end product.
