# Scene-perspective typography — rendered, review pending

**Lifecycle:** [Samad Smett reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@samadsmett/video/7681377548801969426) and [Joshua Esca reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@joshua.esca/video/7563943840009178386) visually sampled → [static-camera grass-plane A/B](../../SCENE-PERSPECTIVE-TYPE-STUDY.md) rendered → human review and moving-plane track pending → mastered **NO**.

The sampled tutorial places text within a room rather than flat over the camera frame. Its caption proposes Premiere's Basic 3D tilt/swivel to match a scene plane and mentions a CapCut equivalent; that CapCut claim was not tested here. A simple fixed tilt is insufficient if the camera or plane moves. Text still needs a justified layer order, consistent perspective, contact/shadow or occlusion where appropriate, mobile readability and brand-safe type.

Joshua's separate 94-second example shows a person covering large title letters and, later, a visible After Effects 3D camera/layer setup and tilted graphic cards. Its caption points to an Instagram tutorial; the sampled TikTok frames do not establish an exact reusable construction. This is broader camera-space composition than a Basic 3D tilt. Do not imply that the two approaches are interchangeable or that an After Effects composition remains editable in another staff editor.

For a real shot, mark the physical surface or depth plane and the point where its perspective changes. Prefer a native editable title plus verified transform/tracking in the chosen staff editor. If the plane moves and the title slides, test planar/corner tracking or keyframes on a protected copy, then reopen/export natively if editable handover is required. Avoid making text appear physically attached to a person or room when the source cannot support a clean track. Check edges and every frame at phone size.

The contained golf example proves only a fixed four-corner perspective on a static-camera shot, with the same moving source frames in both versions. It is not an occlusion or tracking proof, and its bright letters still need a creative texture/grounding judgement. No client footage, native application save/reopen test or moving-plane track has been completed. Keep it experimental until a suitable shot, tracked export, native editability check and human creative approval are witnessed. Do not install a 3D plugin based only on a social caption.

A later [moving boxing-shot source screen](../../SCENE-PERSPECTIVE-TYPE-STUDY.md) rejected the ring mat because an existing sponsor graphic and entering arm occupy the proposed portrait type plane. Inspect negative space before solving tracking; a good track on a bad surface is still a bad effect.
