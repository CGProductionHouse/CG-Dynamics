# Fight pressure pocket

**Lifecycle:** [stock music-gain A/B](../../FIGHT-SCENE4-PRESSURE-POCKET-STUDY.md) proves timing only; production-sound, listening and phone gates pending → mastered **NO**.

Pick a real, timecoded impact with audible room, crowd, footwork, breath or glove sound. Keep the complete musical phrase and camera-audio take available. Make a continuous control before testing a short music reduction in the setup to that impact. The dip should reveal a real sound or create useful anticipation, then resolve with the action. Keep the drop short enough that the trailer retains momentum.

Compare equal-picture, matched-playback-level versions with the same licensed track. Listen for a natural musical entry/return and whether the true contact is clearer. Use only selective licensed effects on a verified visible hit; never use an effect to assert contact hidden by the camera. Preserve separate music, camera sound, ambience and effect tracks for staff revision where possible.

The linked stock test has **no camera audio and no human listening review**. Its 15 dB measured reduction demonstrates a gain envelope, not an effective fight sound design. Validate against the real event and intended phone playback before promotion.
