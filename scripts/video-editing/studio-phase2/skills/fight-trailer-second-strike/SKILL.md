# Fight trailer second strike — blurred-entry practice rejected

**Lifecycle:** [stock-only hard-cut/blur A/B](../../FIGHT-SCENE2-CUT-STUDY.md) → hard cut retained as clearer picture; source pair rejected as a finished trailer sequence → distinct event strike and human playback pending → mastered **NO**.

After choosing the first verified hit, find a *different* source moment with its own bout/clip/timecode evidence. The second moment should register immediately as new energy: changed attack angle, fighter, distance or ring geography. Keep approach and contact visible. A reaction from another bout may be used as a separate montage beat only if the edit does not imply it follows the first blow.

Make a plain hard cut first. Inspect the last four frames of shot A and first four frames of shot B for visual direction, shot scale, gaze and punch path. A short blur, whip or flash is justified only if real source motion gives it a direction and the next action stays readable. Compare at full speed, then check the incoming contact frame and the face/glove area one frame at a time. Reject a transition that merely hides incompatible source, as in the linked four-frame blurred entry.

Keep the sound pass separate: use actual camera sound and a fresh cue only for visible contact. Do not repeat a generic punch sample at every cut. The existing scene 2 A/B is silent, stock-only and shows similar staged boxer close-ups; it does not verify an independent hit, a good trailer rhythm, event continuity or audible quality.

The 29 October [different-source screen](../../FIGHT-SECOND-MOMENT-SOURCE-SCREEN-20261029.md) compares two rights-cleared upright bouts with an immediate hard cut and six-/18-frame explicit research separators. Three outputs decode; the separator revision protects 54 earlier frames. Distinct performers/setting are clearer than in the old similar-boxer pair, but an extended glove reaching a guard is **not** verified landed contact. Six frames are too brief for the disclosure copy; 18 frames interrupt action. Neither slate is a client transition or remedy for missing source truth. Retain the hard cut as a technical control until real event moments and normal-speed/heard/phone/native review exist.
