**Lifecycle:** [owner's Miracamp reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@miracampschool/video/7650495982521634080) sampled at about 2, 15 and 24 s → original masked proof pending → mastered **NO**.

The visible example repeats a basketball player across a jump. Premiere shows `Add Frame Hold` and multiple stepped upper layers. The caption describes duplicating a rotoscoped subject, freezing successive positions and keeping the live subject on the top layer. This is an editing plan, not evidence that natural-footage rotoscoping is automated or that every overlap is clean.

Choose a shot with locked or easily stabilized framing and clear subject separation. Identify a handful of meaningful positions along the action path. On a protected copy, place clean held cutouts at those positions under the current moving subject; avoid excessive clones that obscure the action. Inspect every contact/overlap frame for halos, duplicated shadows, background tears and arbitrary hard edges. Airborne subjects must still have a credible launch and landing in the original continuous shot. Ensure no isolated cutout ends visibly in empty space unless the source action makes that plausible.

Use existing editor-native object mask/roto tools for an editable staff version. No client footage, creator frames, new plugin or AI credits are needed for an initial disposable test. A local natural-footage mask and final export have not yet been verified.
