# Fight trailer cold open — technical proof, human review pending

**Lifecycle:** owner's fight-trailer direction → [stock-only 1.001 s timing A/B](../../FIGHT-SCENE1-IMPACT-STUDY.md) → [silent graphic-detail-to-contact A/B](../../FIGHT-GRAPHIC-RESOLVE-STUDY.md) after owner rejected the wider stock montage/music → original event source and audible review pending → mastered **NO**.

Choose a visually unmistakable contact moment from the complete, authorised event source. Preserve the source file, in/out timecodes and original sound. At original speed, identify approach, actual contact frame and response. Start early enough to read the attack, but cut away once the reaction loses clarity. Do not prolong a slow shot merely because its effect is attractive. Leave enough context that the opening is a true moment, not a fabricated knockout claim.

Render the clean version first. Test a selective sound cue only on the actual visual contact, retaining natural glove/crowd/room sound. Check the SFX licence and exact intended distribution. A waveform peak aligned to a frame proves placement only; compare dry and designed mixes at matched playback level and listen through headphones, speakers and phone. Remove any effect that sounds game-like, exaggerates a miss or muddies the natural hit.

If a shot has a strong foreground detail, test a brief graphic crop that resolves to the full scene **before** contact. The motocross reference uses a high-contrast wheel detail and real camera motion; a boxing adaptation must use an actual glove/body detail and keep the hit unobscured. A posterized look, focus streak or motion blur is optional and should end before the evidence of contact. Keep a plain source control and reject the treatment if it feels like a decorative filter at full speed.

For the next shot, maintain fight geography and distinguish a different moment clearly. Never concatenate unrelated angles to imply one continuous blow or repeat one blow as several. A one-second beat is one building block of the 30-second trailer; prove the handoff into the following shot before calling it a successful opening scene. The linked stock practice has no production sound and no perceptual listening verdict.
