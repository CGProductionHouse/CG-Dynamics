## Scene job and capture

Name the departure, route and arrival or discovery before choosing effects. Obtain at least three *real* views of the same journey: an orienting wide that establishes place/direction, a closer action or viewpoint that advances travel, and a destination/consequence. Keep a recognizable person, wardrobe or object when claiming continuity. Cut on the action or a meaningful view change; do not invent a new geography from unrelated stock or turn three crops of one angle into fake coverage. Retain original location sound, and add music or a removable cue only after the plain progression makes sense.

At portrait/phone size, the subject and travel direction should remain readable in every selected view. If a wider frame crops away the person or the detail becomes a generic building, choose another shot. Preserve source names/timecodes and the cut order for staff revision.
