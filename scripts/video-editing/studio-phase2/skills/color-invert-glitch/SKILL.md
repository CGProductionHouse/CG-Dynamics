# Colour inversion as action punctuation — rejected boxing trial

**Lifecycle:** collection #33 visually sampled → [one-frame stock boxing A/B](../../FIGHT-COLOR-INVERT-STUDY.md) rendered and rejected for that source → full-speed/phone and CG event use untested → mastered **NO**.

The reference shows brief colour-inverted BMX frames on adjustment layers. Its exact settings, audio and suitability for a fight trailer were not verified. Do not treat the inversion as an automatic impact preset.

Before trying it, select the exact source contact and reaction frames, export a plain control, and make the altered span as short and purposeful as the action allows. Check the strike on the normal frames immediately before and after. Compare the whole motion at full speed, not just a still; inspect reduced-size/phone playback and viewer comfort. Preserve the event's true chronology, source sound and skin/brand colours outside the effect.

The current 41.7 ms full-frame negative altered the contact image from dark/cool to near-white and pulled attention away from the glove and face. **Reject that result.** A technically correct one-frame effect is not evidence that it improves a shot. Do not add a subsequent flash or fabricated SFX to compensate for source selection. If the event footage does not offer a visual reason for the effect, leave it out.
