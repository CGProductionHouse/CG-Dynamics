# Exposure flash transition — added pulse rejected in A/B

**Lifecycle:** [Miracamp reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@miracampschool/video/7672852460502895905) sampled at about 7, 20 and 33 s → one stock [flare-cut A/B](../../FLARE-CUT-STUDY.md) rendered; added pulse rejected → mastered **NO**.

The Premiere tutorial shows a nested upper clip crossing a cut, `Levels` and keyframed Transform/Levels settings; the displayed Transform shutter-angle value reaches 200 degrees. This is a short exposure/motion treatment. It is **different from** a matched flash cut built around one repeated object across locations. The exact keyframe curve, output quality and audio were not verified.

A separate [directional flash reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@sam.scherr/video/7677460023420783902) was visually sampled at about 7, 20, 33 and 47 s. It shows an adjustment layer with keyed scale/opacity and `Directional Blur (Legacy)` controls over football footage. Treat this as another family variant, not as confirmation of the Levels recipe above. The sampled frames do not establish exact reusable settings or acceptable full-speed intensity.

Use only where a light source, camera move or musical accent motivates the flash. Start with original rights-cleared adjacent shots; place a protected duplicate of the cut, keyframe the exposure peak and return it quickly to a clean picture. Prefer native editable Levels/Transform controls for staff handover. Check the rendered transition frame by frame for blown faces/logos, unexpected black frames, chromatic halos and jarring repeated flashes. Compare against a plain cut at phone brightness and with sound; keep the plain cut if the flash adds no story value.

No paid pack or plugin is required. The first original local render compares a drummer-to-singer cut where stage lights already flare: an extra +10% pulse raised bright singer pixels from 26.3% to 45.1% and weakened face detail, so the plain cut is preferred. This is negative evidence, not a perfected exposure recipe. No perceptual audio/phone review or approved CG transition has been completed.
