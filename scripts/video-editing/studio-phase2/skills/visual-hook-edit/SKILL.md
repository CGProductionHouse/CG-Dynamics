# Visual hook as an editing decision — reference note, not an effect preset

**Lifecycle:** owner's [problem-to-WAIT example](https://www.tiktok.com/@omgadrian/video/7565322567330942222), [self-referential title example](https://www.tiktok.com/@harrisonniap/video/7645324681960017170), [car-hook commentary](https://www.tiktok.com/@sterling_voth/video/7627277546471689486) and [four-hook presenter](https://www.tiktok.com/@b.erkem/video/7438666366183361824) visually sampled → [first-three-second screen of delivered internal-review films](../../VISUAL-HOOK-FIRST-SECONDS-20261026.md) recorded → controlled original A/B and normal-speed heard review pending → mastered **NO**. Their retention or “viral” claims were not verified.

This is a selection skill, not a template to paste over every film. For an approved brief, identify the freshest relevant visual fact in the available footage: a person doing work, a physical transformation, a surprising detail, a product action or an emotionally specific expression. Compare it against the actual posted client openings before choosing copy or music. Make two short opening candidates from cleared footage: one driven by the strongest natural image, one with a small amount of explanatory type. Judge which establishes the subject and promise fastest *without misleading the viewer*. If neither is good, ask for better source capture or adjust the story; do not invent a generic question already used in the client's feed.

At review, show both candidates at phone size with their first two seconds of sound. Check that the image, title and caption agree, the subject remains visible under platform UI, and the opening flows into the rest of the piece. Record the selected frame/timecode and the reason it serves this particular story. A hook is not accepted merely because a tutorial calls it high-performing.

The [PSG November V1 rejection and V2 revision](../../PSG-NOVEMBER-V2-OWNER-FEEDBACK.md) add a useful client test: write down the viewer, intended feeling and one desired action **before** choosing the hook word. A technically polished footage-in-type reveal failed when its word and solid-colour plate did not serve a reason to contact the client. In the private V2 comparison, the word `PLAN` appears over the moving planting shot and leads into the planning/real-adviser story. It decodes, but no full-speed owner approval is claimed. Preserve specific owner rejections in the source ledger so another agent does not revive an attractive but already failed opening.

The [current opening screen](../../VISUAL-HOOK-FIRST-SECONDS-20261026.md) checks exact exported frames and first-two-second decoded levels rather than guessing from a source thumbnail. It confirms the opening propositions and flags the quieter TBS first ten seconds for staff *listening*, without treating RMS as a creative verdict or touching the already delivered packages. This is a review habit, not proof that either hook performs.
