# Heat lens push — source-bound candidate

The owner reference is [`@sundawave` Moments in Motion](https://www.tiktok.com/@sundawave/video/7701583122197957896). Sampled opening playback shows a brief lens-like radial/chromatic push on moving aerial picture before a close-up. The exact post's CapCut anchor led to a generic editor page; reference audio and editable transition settings were unavailable. The [4 November study](../../EHRLICH-TWO-CAPCUT-REFERENCE-STUDIES-20261104.md) records the exact observation and limits.

## Construction

1. Select a motivated outgoing texture or movement, such as flame/heat, that can absorb a short optical distortion. Choose a destination action that stays legible and attractive without the effect.
2. Make a hard-cut control at the same source time. Apply the optical pulse only to the last few outgoing frames. Keep its centre tied to the actual subject or action. Release to a **clean** destination frame; do not let the warp or channel split smear product, faces, brand marks or text after the cut.
3. Inspect at full and phone size around the exact boundary. Compare frame-to-frame motion and post-cut pixel identity, then assess whether the pulse helps the source story at normal speed.
4. Fit licensed sound to the optical event on a separate stem. Measure onset, peak, decay, true peak and delivered-file decode; listen independently before judging creative fit. Do not treat generic whoosh alignment as replication of a muted reference.

## Tested result and failure

`../../ehrlich-november/reference_proofs_20261104.py` builds a 3 s EHP fire-to-knife hard-cut/pulse pair from verified original footage plus licensed fire and sound. The first variant distorted the incoming meat and was rejected. The repaired variant confines radial/chromatic remap to frames before the 1.20 s cut; destination frames were confirmed identical to the hard-cut picture. Its decoded AAC cue peak was 1.22 s, within one 30 fps frame of the cut. Still inspection shows the flame remains legible. Normal-speed creative sound and whole-film value are **unverified**; this is not a default transition.

## Use gate

Use only if the outgoing image supports the optical idea and the pulse adds anticipation without obscuring the arriving action. Reject if the effect makes food unappetising, moves the horizon without reason, hides contact or is indistinguishable from a simpler cut in context. Keep native editable reconstruction and client export review separate from this rendered proof.
