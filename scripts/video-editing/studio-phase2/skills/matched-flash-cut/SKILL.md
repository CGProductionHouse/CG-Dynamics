# Matched flash cut — researched, untested

**Lifecycle:** owner's [Arthur Cyiza reference](https://www.tiktok.com/@iam_arthur7/video/7394863028220513579) visually sampled and caption read → [two-key source screen](../../MATCHED-KEY-SOURCE-SCREEN-20261026.md) rejects a semantic-only match → original matching three-take capture/export and human review pending → mastered **NO**.

The visible example holds a phone with text as a stable centre anchor while the surroundings change. The creator's method is to choose the object and locations before filming, use framing grids, record several seconds in at least three places, then trim and align overlapping clips by reduced opacity before making very short cuts and adding a restrained sound accent. The exact cut length and musical timing were not heard or measured here.

For a CG job, choose a repeated client-relevant object or gesture that advances one clear message. Request or screen at least three **original, cleared** takes with a near-identical anchor position, scale, orientation and direction of travel. Align the anchor on adjacent frames before shortening the cuts. Inspect phone-size playback for a stable focal point, readable text and absence of exposure/colour jumps. Use the existing editor or FFmpeg for ordinary cuts; the technique does not justify a new plugin. Keep any isolated foreground body/object grounded in the frame or naturally occluded.

This card has no rendered CG proof and is not permission to reuse the creator's frames, music or client footage. Promote only after a real multi-shot source set, exact output review, sound audition and CG creative approval. Do not confuse a quick montage of unrelated shots with a matched flash cut.

The [rejected local pair](../../MATCHED-KEY-SOURCE-SCREEN-20261026.md) explains the gate: a black car fob/pouch on a counter and a small blue-tagged house key at a door do not make one visually stable anchor, even though both filenames contain “key”. The 9:16 crop makes their size/position/action mismatch obvious. Do not force alignment with extreme zoom, blur or a flash.
