**Lifecycle:** [24 fps stock natural/hold A/B](../../FIGHT-SCENE3-CONTACT-HOLD-STUDY.md) → two-frame repeat rejected for this slowed source → actual event and sound/phone review pending → mastered **NO**.

When a power-run hit feels too quick, first find the real contact and ensure the shot is legible at original speed. Check source frame rate and whether the camera already baked in slow motion. Make a continuous control before trying a freeze, speed ramp, impact shake or exposure accent. Never use those accents to hide a missed or blocked strike.

If the source truly needs emphasis, hold only a sharp frame with genuine contact, preserve the start of the response, and test the shortest visible duration. Inspect the frames before contact, the held frame and the next motion step. A duplicated frame can read as a playback fault, especially in footage already slowed to 24 fps. Compare both versions at normal speed and phone size with the same sound. Keep natural glove/crowd sound available and do not add a sample solely to justify the effect.
