# Fight foreground handoff — stock cut timing tested

**Stage:** [silent two-source glove-sweep A/B](../../FIGHT-GLOVE-HANDOFF-STUDY.md) rendered; event use and human full-speed/phone review pending. Mastered: **no**.

Search the *source footage* for a glove, rope, official or crowd member passing close to lens and visibly anchored to a frame edge. Check the next shot for a compatible motion direction and enough fighter/geography context after the cut. A real foreground pass can carry energy; do not add blur merely because the reference contains moving dirt or riders. Keep a plain earlier-cut control and cut on the source motion peak. Inspect three frames before and after the cut at native size and on a phone.

Check event truth before adopting the cut. Two bouts or strikes must not be made to look like one continuous landed punch. Keep original moment IDs and distinguish a new moment with angle, sound, or breathing room when needed. Do not let foreground obscure the contact evidence. The existing stock A/B uses different performers, lacks camera sound and never proves a landed hit; its current form is not a client trailer scene. Finish with an audible, full-speed review using original source sound and a staff-revisable timeline.
