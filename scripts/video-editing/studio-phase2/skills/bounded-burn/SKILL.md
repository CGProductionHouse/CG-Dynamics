**Reject the candidate for an edit.** The contact frames show a narrow, mechanical vertical stripe. It neither adds meaningful story information nor looks like a physical film exposure. Next gate: only consider a real, rights-cleared burn whose image texture and transition purpose fit a selected shot pair. Do not automatically add it to client films to make them appear more elaborate.
