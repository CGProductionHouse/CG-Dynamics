# Visible contact → sound candidate

**Stage: rendered technical candidate; audible/phone review pending.** View the [music-only control](proof-cup-contact-control.mp4), [contact-sound candidate](proof-cup-contact-candidate.mp4) and [12-frame motion contact](proof-cup-contact-frames.png). This is a 3 s stock-only sound-placement exercise, not a client edit. It follows the [rejected sip cue](SOUND-CUT-STUDY.md) by choosing an action that the footage actually shows.

The [Mixkit green-mug take](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/coffee-in-green-mug-818/) shows hands lowering a cup and saucer onto a surface and releasing it. A tight portrait crop would cut the hands, so this study keeps the complete 1280×720 landscape frame. The available source has no camera-audio stream. The candidate adds Mixkit's `Dish placed in a wood table` effect from its [restaurant sounds](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/restaurant/); the possible wood/table timbre versus the shot's dark surface needs listening review. Both variants share the same placeholder `Cat Walk` music excerpt, which has not been approved for this scene. [The manifest](cup-contact-study.json) records source frames, file hashes, official [video](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/), [music](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/) and [SFX](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/) licence links. Raw stock assets stay in ignored `media/`.

The selected source range is frames 106–177 inclusive at 24 fps. Denser 8 fps frame inspection estimated contact around source frame 142, or 1.50 s into this cut. The candidate cue starts at 1.45 s; the source effect's measured first RMS peak is around 0.05 s after its start. An initial 1.28 s onset was visibly early relative to the saucer's descent and was corrected before saving this proof. The [small renderer](render_cup_contact.py) checks file hashes and source-frame bounds and preserves the encoded picture when changing audio.

Both saved MP4s fully decode at 1280×720, 24 fps, 72 video frames and exactly 3.000 s, H.264/AAC. Their encoded video-stream SHA-256 matches: `44baf1ce33e3586d56f78149cd517830a6d5ee71b10a17517471c65dd5d8a418`. The music-only control measures −20.34 LUFS integrated / −5.71 dBTP; the candidate measures −20.18 LUFS / −5.71 dBTP. This checks similar overall level and headroom. It cannot prove that the effect sounds like this cup, that its attack is perceptually synchronized, or that the placeholder music fits. No full-speed listening on speakers, headphones or a phone has been witnessed.

**Decision:** keep the candidate for a human A/B listen, with the music-only version as the clean fallback. Do not call the sound-led skill mastered or use this as a universal cup effect. If the sound is too wooden, hard, early, late or cartoonish, reject it and use genuine camera sound or a better matched, licensed recording when available. A sound cue has to fit the *visible material and motion*, not only land on the correct frame.
