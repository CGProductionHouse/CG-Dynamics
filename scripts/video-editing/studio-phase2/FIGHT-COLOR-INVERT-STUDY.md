- [Reproduce](render_fight_color_invert.py): run `python render_fight_color_invert.py` in this folder with the hash-checked source. Both branches take the first 48 frames at 24000/1001 fps, crop x=300–704 to portrait, scale to 540×960. The revision applies FFmpeg `negate=enable='eq(n,8)'` for **one 41.7 ms frame**. The script combines them with conspicuous stock-practice labels.

The altered frame is early in the visibly contacting glove sequence. The normal frame's mean Y was 58.1; the inverted frame's mean Y was 199.0, with the share of Y≥235 pixels rising from 0.01% to 12.97%. In the inspected still, the treatment washes out the glove, face and skin and changes the shot's palette abruptly. The original contact and reaction are already legible without it. This is not a useful emphasis for this source. The contact sheet tests several neighbouring frames; it does **not** establish how the 41.7 ms flash feels at full speed or whether a physical phone flags it as uncomfortable. Do not ship it as a tested Fight Night effect, infer a safe flash frequency, or hide a genuine event impact with it.
