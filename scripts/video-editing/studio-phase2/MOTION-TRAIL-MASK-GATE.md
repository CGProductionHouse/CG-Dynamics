# Natural-footage motion trail: mask gate rejected

The [motion-trail card](skills/motion-trail-clones/SKILL.md) had rejected a near-static cup. This follow-up screened two different source choices before trying to render a full video.

The existing [Mixkit green-screen dancer #28289](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/boy-dancing-funnily-on-a-green-chroma-background-28289/) changes poses over roughly source 3–7 s but stays near the same horizontal position. His truncated lower body meets the source frame edge, so it is not an independently floating mask; however, separated held positions would require inventing lateral travel. No clone was made from that source.

The new [Mixkit skater #34382](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/boy-doing-a-pirouette-with-a-skateboard-in-a-park-34382/) is under the [Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/). Its ignored local file has SHA-256 `3472f2ef04d34632eec0d024533ef4d6346adb38e712a6c4d4dcf41318fd2892`, 1280×720, 24000/1001 fps, 9.092 s, and no audio stream. At source ≈5.2, 6.5 and 7.8 s the skater actually travels across the ramp; the board, wheels and shadow give a physical contact reference. This is a suitable *movement path*, though the background is dense with trees.

A disposable single-frame test held the ≈5.2 s rider under the ≈6.5 s live frame at 45% opacity, using OpenCV GrabCut with manually constrained subject/board regions. The [rejected same-frame A/B](proof-motion-trail-rejected.png) shows the problem: tree texture leaks into the cutout, the skateboarding silhouette has holes, and the feet-to-board connection is broken. That is the floating/detached-edge failure the owner flagged. A second rough manual correction did not remove it. **Do not present this as an effect proof, reuse the mask, or render an animated trail from it.**

The next valid test needs a clean subject matte that includes shoes, skateboard, wheels and contact/shadow, checked at original size across the entire action. Prefer an editor-native object mask/roto on a protected source or an actually clean plate. Keep held positions at the source's real coordinates; do not shift a stationary dancer sideways just to manufacture a trail. Compare the plain shot and the clone treatment at normal speed and phone size before promoting the card. No client media, plugin, external upload, music or AI credit was used here.
