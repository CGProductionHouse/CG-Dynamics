# Footage-inside-type opening: phone-size fill A/B

**Decision: brighter fill alone is insufficient.** This revisits the first 1.25 s of the existing `MOVE` footage-inside-type proof, not a new client video. The [side-by-side video](proof-matte-readability-ab.mp4) and [six-phase contact](proof-matte-readability-contact.jpg) show the same moving subject, word mask and 24 fps timing at **270×480 per variant**. The left panel uses the original `#143448` scene fill; the right uses `#427B98`. No sound is included in this visual comparison.

At 270×480, `MOVE` remains legible in sampled frames, and the brighter version separates its letters a little better from the almost-black background. But the opening still occupies a small central area, and the moving person's face/body are only fragments inside thin glyph regions before the push. The bigger creative issue is subject-through-letter visibility and the opening's scale, not just the background value. The brighter colour does not earn a revised master. By the start of the push the enlarged word quickly leaves the screen, which is the intended aperture mechanism; this short A/B does not review the full 4.5 s move or its sound.

Reproduce with `python render_reference_matte.py` to generate the ignored original mask frames, then `python render_matte_readability_ab.py`. The [A/B script](render_matte_readability_ab.py) checks the Mixkit #28306 source SHA-256 from `project.json` and the first 30 matte-frame digest `b30df88c164301a105c4b250ad3f601435c0a804da159f0e2ee850e5b38551db`. The source is [Mixkit #28306](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/girl-dancing-with-her-earphones-on-a-green-background-28306/) under its [Free Stock Video License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/); the original remains ignored. The 540×480 H.264 A/B has 30 frames/1.250 s, fully decodes, and SHA-256 `345783fe0a8105a2c32d4569417b681b5679c1d8252bd3a722aa51635372a52c`.

This is a **phone-resolution proxy viewed as an image contact**, not a physical phone, full-speed perceptual approval or sound review. A next visual test should first pick a shot/action that remains recognizable through the letters, then compare opening word scale and dwell with an unchanged control. Protect the already-corrected bottom-frame anchoring. Do not promote a colour tweak as a mastered track-matte skill or assume a staff editor can revise the flat MP4 text.
