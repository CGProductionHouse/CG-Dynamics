## Comparison and limits

Both variants use source frames **300–539** and 120 output frames at **30000/1001 fps** (4.004 s). Constant speed selects successive source frames roughly two apart. The ramp integrates a smooth speed curve: slower before about 1.1 s, faster through the central action, slower again after about 2.9 s, normalized to the same source endpoint. Example output-frame mapping: at frame 30, constant selects source 360 versus ramp 336; at frame 60 both select 421; at frame 90 constant selects 481 versus ramp 504. Both maps are strictly increasing, with no repeated source frame; adjacent source steps are 2–3 for constant and 1–4 for ramp. This is sample selection, **not optical-flow interpolation**. A four-frame step in the fast section can still appear staccato at full speed; no perceptual playback was witnessed.

The green background is intentionally left visible so the test isolates temporal pacing. It is not a finished composition. The contact frames show different pose timing and common endpoints; they cannot establish that the ramp feels musical, smooth or better. Both separate exports and the comparison fully decode as H.264 video without audio.
