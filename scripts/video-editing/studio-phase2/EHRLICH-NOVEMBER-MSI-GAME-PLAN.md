## Music and sound decision

Target: serious, cinematic, physical pulse with enough space for ignition/sizzle and a resolving end. Reject happy/family, Viking/ritual, horror and previously used/rejected tracks. Audition at least two actual candidate files against the first 10 s and ending before reserving one in the shared tracker. Mixkit's cinematic catalogue is a discovery source, **not** an editorial verdict. Under its Free SFX license, commercial end-product use is allowed, but the raw items cannot be redistributed as a shared stock pack. `sfx-library.md` is the office-visible discovery/registration catalog; the edit must store cue ID, URL, license, exact timeline hit, gain and stem path in its private manifest. Prefer original camera fire/knife/sizzle when clean; mute crew chatter.

Sound stems: source action/ambience, licensed foley, transitions, music, and optional title accent. Audition each layer and the composite; align transient peaks to picture. Verify mono and stereo, true peak/loudness, full-speed headphone/phone listening and the exact last five seconds. A decode, waveform or model measurement is not creative listening clearance.
