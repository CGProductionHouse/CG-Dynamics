## Using these in real films

Begin with the existing client library and approved edits, not a fresh brand hunt. Choose distinct jobs: camera to connect service detail, outline to preserve a readable title as a real person passes, prelap to anticipate the next visible subject/action, inversion for a fitting sports/product accent. TBS usually benefits from the restrained camera example, not the sport invert burst. These are choices, not a requirement to paste all five into every film.

Write actual frame ranges, story purpose, operator/card/implementation hashes, source/matte/font bindings, parameters and sound markers into `skill_applications` in the existing production plan. The renderer must use those same objects and emit its actual manifest. Export the untreated control from the same source/time/layout; bind its decoded evidence. Read [production execution](../../../.agents/skills/cg-video-story-editor/references/production-execution.md). Do not record a “skill used” because its name appears in a script or a review form.

For every planned caption/title, choose its entrance, readable hold, interaction with action and exit; apply approved brand font and real safe-area/crop views. Use the outline construction when its moving foreground and contrast support it, not as the same default across clients. For every move/cut, choose a real sound job (including deliberate quiet), inspect the actual export and audition its exact mix through a proven route. Beat placement alone does not prove good music.

For flash/invert, keep the treatment brief and purposeful, preserve a clean version and review contrast/comfort in motion at full and phone size. Repetition can be unpleasant; these tests are not a photosensitivity certification. Do not cover the product/action payoff or use a flash to hide bad cuts, shaky footage or failed masks.

**Remaining gates:** MSI audition/import of the TBS insert; native AE/Resolve/Premiere reproduction for these new code operators; physical filmed last-frame capture; genuine different-shot natural prelap; client-specific creative/source/phone acceptance. All are explicit, not waived by unit tests or render counts. No client sends, MSI timeline changes, reference-asset redistribution or resumption of the rejected PC batch.
