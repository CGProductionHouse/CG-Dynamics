# Current-session audio input route check

27 October 2026. A repeated current-session transport check asked whether Codex could receive actual audio through the tool's audio-content output. Earlier [sound cards](skills/sound-led-cuts/SKILL.md) had already recorded omitted audio, so this is a reconfirmation of a known limitation, not a new listening capability or a sound-skill milestone. No local Gemma variant was rerun.

Three one-second, 24 kHz mono original controls were generated privately: silence, a steady 440 Hz tone with short fades, and a short broadband/low-tone transient. Their order was randomized, with the mapping saved privately before presentation. The tool emitted controls A/B/C without their truth labels. For **all three**, the returned result explicitly stated: `audio content omitted because you do not support audio input`.

**Result: unsupported transport, no listening verdict.** No control was classified and no client or third-party sound was uploaded. The truth mapping was not used to invent an audition result. Original WAVs and the mapping remain under the private `Nolan-Skill-Proofs/audio-input-route-20261027` directory. This finding applies to this session/tool route; it does not claim every future model or host has the same capabilities.

Continue autonomous timing, source classification, clipping/true-peak, loudness, stereo/mono, fade, stem alignment and delivered-file tests. Keep creative music/SFX fit and independent full-speed listening explicit. The owner is not a routine practice-audition gate. Do not repeat these controls in the same unsupported route or rerun failed Gemma variants without a concrete capability change. A waveform or a known truth label is not hearing.
