# Masked-opening zoom: source suitability screen

The [masked-zoom skill](skills/masked-zoom-transition/SKILL.md) is still **research**, not rendered. This screen checks whether an actual filmed opening supports a transition before drawing a mask around a shape that merely looks circular.

The existing [Mixkit cup #818](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/coffee-in-green-mug-818/) is a poor transition aperture. The 2 fps overview of its full 10.25 s shows a narrow ellipse of opaque latte art at the cup top. Replacing that surface with a new scene would demand a story reason and a convincing liquid-to-scene handoff; simply cutting through the ellipse would look like a pasted video. Do not reuse this cup merely because it is already local.

I then screened [Mixkit magnifying-glass/book #13807](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/hand-of-a-person-using-a-magnifying-glass-to-read-13807/) under the [Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/). Its ignored local file has SHA-256 `e89e671250c33c37732708bd97009db253c13c4870f2672fd90f5199f2c7085f`, 1280×720, 24000/1001 fps, 7.007 s, and **no audio stream**. A full-source 3 fps overview and a 2.5 s native frame show a real, dark-rimmed lens opening crossing readable book detail. The lens and hand move, so a mask must follow the inner rim frame by frame; a fixed circle will slip. The rim/hand must stay on top of incoming footage and the mask edge must stay attached to them until an optical push fills the frame. This source passes the *physical-opening* screen, but the page/book continuity and matched incoming shot are still undecided.

No transition was rendered: there is no justified second shot yet, and a copied book paragraph or unrelated coffee clip would add a false narrative jump. A valid next A/B should compare a plain zoom/cut with a tracked lens aperture using two cleared, causally related shots. Inspect every mask frame, incoming fill, edge blur and the final frame at full size and phone size. Do not call a source screen a working masked zoom skill or claim sound from these silent files.
