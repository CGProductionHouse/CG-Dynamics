## 30-second construction to test, not a preset

- **0–3 s:** strongest readable real impact or a tension beat that immediately promises one. Establish the fighter/action, not an unrelated title or borrowed reference footage.
- **3–8 s:** brief anticipation: footwork, guard, crowd, bell or a boxer preparing. Let the eye understand ring orientation.
- **8–23 s:** escalating clean, distinct moments. Keep the contact frame readable; use motion-matched cuts when punch direction and screen geography genuinely align. Let a high-frame-rate shot breathe before and after the hit rather than slowing the entire sequence.
- **23–30 s:** the strongest *verified* moment, its immediate consequence, then a concise event identity/end button. Change this structure if the actual footage suggests a better arc.

Start with a straight-cut picture-only assembly. Compare a sound-designed version at matched playback level. Natural camera/ring audio is the first candidate for glove impact, breathing, shoes, bell and crowd. Layer individually licensed SFX only when they clarify a visible moment; a bass hit or sub-drop cannot turn a near-miss into a landed punch. Leave micro-silence before a peak if it heightens the real sound. Put production sound, ambience, music and effects on separate named tracks, with a timing/gain/source manifest for any offline render. Use an approved music licence for the intended client/social distribution; a TikTok reference's soundtrack is not a licence.

For a free candidate, Mixkit's current [stock music licence](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/) lists social posts and online ads as permitted uses, and its separate [SFX licence](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/sfxFree/) permits effects in commercial/social end products while barring stock redistribution. Check each exact asset and destination before use; no Fight Night track or effect has been selected. Freesound assets have [different per-file CC0, CC BY or CC BY-NC terms](https://freesound.org/help/faq/), so a “free download” is not enough for commercial use. YouTube Creator Music is a poor default for this cross-platform fight edit: [availability and usage terms vary](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11610212?hl=en), and its [restricted categories include violence](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11623091?hl=en).

Speed changes require an actual source-frame check. High-frame-rate material can provide clean slow motion; interpolation around fast gloves/faces can warp contact. Adobe's [frame-rate guidance](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/modify-clip-properties/change-the-frame-rate-of-a-clip.html) and [interpolation guidance](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/change-clip-speed/apply-time-interpolation-methods-to-adjust-clip-speed.html) distinguish source interpretation, sampling, blending and optical flow. The existing [local speed-ramp proof](SPEED-RAMP-STUDY.md) establishes framewise timing on stock footage only. Adobe's [Essential Sound types](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/premiere/desktop/add-audio-effects/adjust-volume-and-levels/audio-editing-with-essential-sound-panel.html) and [ducking controls](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/premiere/desktop/add-audio-effects/adjust-volume-and-levels/automatically-duck-audio.html) provide native options if this edit is handed to a Premiere editor; the final format stays tool-agnostic.

## Acceptance before calling it a fight highlight

- A second pass at original speed confirms each represented landed blow against its source timecode. Preserve a contact-evidence log; if unclear, label it atmosphere or omit it.
- Review the full 30 seconds with sound on speakers and headphones, then at phone size. Confirm punch clarity, cut rhythm, music/SFX fit, no misleading repetition, title safe areas and a clean ending.
- Verify export duration, video/audio streams, full decode and beginning/ending frames. A technical pass is separate from a human creative approval.
- Record what edit format staff can revise, where the original footage and licences live, and how to reproduce the export. Do not call a flat MP4 staff-editable.
