## Sound timing and acceptance limits

The first mix trimmed Mixkit #1492 at 0.8 s, but a 20 ms RMS analysis located its actual peak at 1.06 s: the cue's strongest event was missing. The repair retains 1.33 s and schedules its peak on the visible change. On the decoded AAC outputs, the strongest 20 ms windows were **1.52 s** for the pane release at 1.50 s and **1.22 s** for the lens cut at 1.20 s, within one 30 fps frame. Both designed exports decoded completely. This establishes cue alignment only. It does **not** establish that the sound is creatively suitable or that the reference's actual audio was replicated; no qualified normal-speed hearing of the exact mix occurred on this MSI.
